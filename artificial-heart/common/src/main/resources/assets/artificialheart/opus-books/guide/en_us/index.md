---
title: Artificial Heart
---
Welcome to the Artificial Heart guide. This mod gives you the look of the pale garden without the spooky side
effects: creaking hearts that never spawn creakings, eyeblossoms that stay open or closed, and pale pumpkins that
can even build you a friendly creaking golem.

## Where to start

* [Pale garden decor](01-pale-garden-decor/index.md): the Detached Creaking Heart and the clipped eyeblossoms.
* [Pale pumpkins](02-pale-pumpkins/index.md): growing, carving, and lighting pale pumpkins.
* [Creaking golems](03-creaking-golems.md): build a creaking that will never hurt you.
* [Advancements](04-advancements.md): two goals to work toward.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a pale pumpkin.
