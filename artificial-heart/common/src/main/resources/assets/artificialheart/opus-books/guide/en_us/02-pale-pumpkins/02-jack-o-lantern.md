Craft a Pale Carved Pumpkin with a torch to make a Pale Jack o'Lantern.

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [artificialheart:pale_carved_pumpkin, torch]
result: artificialheart:pale_jack_o_lantern
```

```item
id: artificialheart:pale_jack_o_lantern
label: Pale Jack o'Lantern
```

It can top a [creaking golem](../03-creaking-golems.md) just like the plain carved pumpkin.
