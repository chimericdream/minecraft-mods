## Finding them

Pale pumpkin patches grow naturally in Pale Garden biomes, on grass or pale moss.

## Farming

Craft a pale pumpkin into 4 pale pumpkin seeds, or get seeds by carving one (see below). Plant the seeds and the
stem grows pale pumpkins, just like regular pumpkins.

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [artificialheart:pale_pumpkin]
result: artificialheart:pale_pumpkin_seeds
count: 4
```

Pale pumpkin seeds are in the Natural Blocks creative tab.

## Carving

Use shears on a pale pumpkin. It becomes a **Pale Carved Pumpkin** facing you (or the side you clicked, if it
was a side face), gives 4 pale pumpkin seeds, and the shears lose 1 durability.

```item
id: artificialheart:pale_carved_pumpkin
label: Pale Carved Pumpkin
```

## Wearing

A Pale Carved Pumpkin can be worn as a helmet. While you wear one, no creaking will notice you, including
[creaking golems](../03-creaking-golems.md).

> [!NOTE]
> A dispenser aimed at a finished golem frame places the carved pumpkin on top and completes the golem. Otherwise it
> dresses the pumpkin onto a nearby mob like a vanilla carved pumpkin.
