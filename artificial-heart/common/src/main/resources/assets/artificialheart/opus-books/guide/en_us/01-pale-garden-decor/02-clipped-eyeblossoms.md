Vanilla eyeblossoms open and close with the time of day. Clipped eyeblossoms stay exactly how you left them.

```item
id: artificialheart:clipped_eyeblossom
label: Clipped Eyeblossom
```

```item
id: artificialheart:clipped_open_eyeblossom
label: Clipped Open Eyeblossom
```

## Getting them

Use shears on a vanilla eyeblossom. A closed one becomes a **Clipped Eyeblossom** and an open one becomes a
**Clipped Open Eyeblossom**. The shears lose 1 durability.

Both are in the Building Blocks creative tab.

## Using them

* Place them like any small flower, or put them in a flower pot.

## Dyes

Craft a clipped flower on its own to get a dye.

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [artificialheart:clipped_eyeblossom]
result: gray_dye
```

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [artificialheart:clipped_open_eyeblossom]
result: orange_dye
```
