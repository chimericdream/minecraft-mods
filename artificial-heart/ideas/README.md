Feature ideas for this mod, from first thought to ready to build. The process is described in
[`docs/FEATURE-WORK.md`](../../docs/FEATURE-WORK.md).

## Identity

> **The pale garden, built with.** Artificial Heart began as a decorative creaking heart and is the
> home for pale garden content: tamed versions of its blocks, plus pumpkins, creakings, and whatever
> else suits the biome. Ideas can be any size, from a one-block variant to a new mechanic or mob, but
> they must belong to the pale garden itself, not to a general "tamed blocks" theme. The recurring
> pattern is "all the atmosphere, none of the side effects" (a block that looks right without its
> vanilla behavior getting in the way), but it's a pattern, not a limit.
