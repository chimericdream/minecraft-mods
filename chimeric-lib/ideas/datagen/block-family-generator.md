# Block family generator

> Source: legacy backlog, [§ Registration & data generation](../brainstorms/2026-08-27-legacy/potential-features.md#registration--data-generation) · Status: **Exploring**

## Description

Declare a base block once and auto-generate stairs, slabs, walls, vertical
variants, plus their models, blockstates, recipes, loot tables, and tags (Minekea's bread and butter,
useful to every content mod).
