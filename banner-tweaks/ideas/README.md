# Banner Tweaks — Ideas

Feature ideas for this mod, from first thought to ready to build. The process is described in
[`docs/FEATURE-WORK.md`](../../docs/FEATURE-WORK.md).

## Identity

> **Banners, end to end.** Banner Tweaks makes banners easier to make, copy, display, and place: more
> layers, a better loom, shareable designs, new shapes, and new ways to hang them. It stays
> vanilla-friendly, so ideas should feel like a natural extension of what banners already are.
> Generated banners at structures are in scope only if the results reliably look good. A random pile of
> patterns doesn't qualify, so any such idea has to explain how it avoids ugly output (curated
> templates, constrained palettes and pattern rules, or hand-authored sets).

## Active ideas

The legacy backlog was shortlisted in
[`brainstorms/2026-08-13-legacy/shortlist.md`](brainstorms/2026-08-13-legacy/shortlist.md).

### Copying and sharing

- [Copy banners regardless of layer count](copying-and-sharing/copy-any-layer-count.md) — Banners above six
  layers can still be copied onto a blank banner.
- [Banner stencil](copying-and-sharing/banner-stencil.md) — Save a banner's pattern list onto a stencil and
  apply it to any blank banner.
- [Shield parity](copying-and-sharing/shield-parity.md) — The raised layer limit applies to shields too.
- [Copy a placed banner in-world](copying-and-sharing/copy-placed-banner.md) — Sneak-use a blank banner on
  a placed banner to copy it.

### Loom

- [Advanced loom: layer list editor](loom/advanced-loom.md) — A new block with a layer panel to delete,
  reorder and recolor layers.
- [Bigger loom preview](loom/bigger-loom-preview.md) — Hover-to-zoom or a side preview for dense designs.

### Placement

- [Hang banners from the underside of blocks](placement/underside-banners.md) — Banners hanging down from
  a ceiling.
- [Horizontal banners](placement/horizontal-banners.md) — Banners placed horizontally; what that means is
  still to be decided.

### Shapes

- [Banner shape variants](shapes/banner-shape-variants.md) — Jagged, sharp and pennant banner shapes.

### Structures

- [Curated banners for structures](structures/curated-structure-banners.md) — Hand-authored banner sets
  that appear in structures.

### Configuration

- [Separate limits for crafting and commands](configuration/separate-crafting-and-command-limits.md) — A
  different layer cap for loom crafting than for commands and datapacks.
