**Gentle Touch** is an enchantment for brushes. Each time you finish brushing a block, there is a small chance you get
the drop and the block resets with a fresh item to dig up, instead of turning into its normal block.

| Level | Chance per finished block |
|:------|:--------------------------|
| I | 2% |
| II | 4% |
| III | 6% |

* It works on this mod's suspicious blocks and on vanilla suspicious sand and gravel.
* It can show up at an enchanting table, and you can also add it to a brush on an anvil.
* Getting a bonus drop earns the **Lucky Block** [advancement](06-advancements.md).
