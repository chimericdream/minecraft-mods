---
title: Hiding your own loot
icon: item:minecraft:suspicious_sand
tags: [tips]
summary: Bury an item in a block for someone to brush up.
---
You can turn a normal block into a suspicious one with an item of your choice inside.

1. Put a **brush in your off hand**.
2. Hold the item you want to hide in your main hand.
3. Right-click one of the blocks listed below.

The block becomes its suspicious version, and one of the item you held goes inside. Brush it later to get the item back.

These blocks can hide an item: clay, dirt, gravel, mud, packed mud, red sand, rooted dirt, sand, soul sand, and soul
soil. Sand and gravel become vanilla suspicious sand and suspicious gravel.

> [!NOTE]
> You need an item in your main hand, and one of it is used up.
