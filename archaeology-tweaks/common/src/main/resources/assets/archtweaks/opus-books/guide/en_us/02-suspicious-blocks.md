Each block below looks like its normal version at first glance. All of them are in the Functional Blocks creative tab.
Each hides one item, picked at random from its own list. Every list can be changed by a datapack, see
[Customizing loot](07-customizing-loot.md).

## Where they generate

They show up as small, rare patches when the world is created:

| Block | Where |
|:------|:------|
| Suspicious Clay | Rivers and beaches |
| Suspicious Dirt | Forests, taigas, and plains |
| Suspicious Mud | Swamps and mangrove swamps |
| Suspicious Packed Mud | Mangrove swamps |
| Suspicious Red Sand | Badlands |
| Suspicious Rooted Dirt | Azalea tree roots (see the notes below) |
| Suspicious Soul Sand | The Nether |
| Suspicious Soul Soil | The Nether |

## What they hold

| Block | Common finds | Rare finds |
|:------|:-------------|:-----------|
| Suspicious Clay | clay ball, bone, flint, string | emerald, angler and flow sherds |
| Suspicious Dirt | stick, bone meal, wheat seeds, flint | emerald, sheaf and howl sherds |
| Suspicious Mud | slime ball, lily pad, vine, bone | emerald, mourner sherd |
| Suspicious Packed Mud | mud bricks, brick, clay ball | amethyst shard, sheaf and shelter sherds |
| Suspicious Red Sand | gold nugget, terracotta, cactus, bone | emerald, archer and danger sherds |
| Suspicious Rooted Dirt | flowering azalea, moss carpet, bone meal | amethyst shard, plenty and sheaf sherds |
| Suspicious Soul Sand | bone, gold nugget, quartz | blaze powder, skull and burn sherds |
| Suspicious Soul Soil | bone, gold nugget, nether wart | glowstone dust, howl and heartbreak sherds |

## Block notes

* **Suspicious Red Sand** falls like sand. The others stay put if the block under them is removed.
* **Suspicious Soul Sand** slows you down, just like soul sand.
* **Suspicious Rooted Dirt** takes bone meal when there is air below it, which grows hanging roots there.
* Azalea tree roots have a small chance (about 2%) to place Suspicious Rooted Dirt instead of normal rooted dirt. This
  covers Lush Caves ceiling trees and azalea trees you grow with bone meal.
* A piston pushing one of these blocks breaks it.
