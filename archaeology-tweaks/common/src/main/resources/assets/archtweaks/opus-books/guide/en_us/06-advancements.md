Look for the **Archaeology Tweaks** tab in your advancements.

| Advancement | How to earn it |
|:------------|:---------------|
| First Dig | Use a brush on a suspicious block for the first time. |
| Preservationist | Collect every pottery sherd. A challenge. |
| Interdimensional Archaeology | Brush a suspicious block in the Nether. |
| Lucky Block | Get a second drop from a block with [Gentle Touch](04-gentle-touch.md). |
