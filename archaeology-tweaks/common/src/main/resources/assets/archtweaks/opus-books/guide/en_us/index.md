Welcome to the Archaeology Tweaks guide. Vanilla archaeology only has suspicious sand and suspicious gravel. This mod
adds more suspicious blocks, so there is something to dig up in many more places.

## Where to start

* [Brushing](01-brushing.md): how to dig up a suspicious block.
* [Suspicious blocks](02-suspicious-blocks.md): every block, where it generates, and what it can hold.
* [Hiding your own loot](03-hiding-items.md): bury an item for someone to find.
* [Gentle Touch](04-gentle-touch.md): a brush enchantment that can give a second drop.
* [Pottery sherds](05-pottery-sherds.md): trading with the wandering trader.
* [Advancements](06-advancements.md): a few goals to work toward.
* [Customizing loot](07-customizing-loot.md): for pack makers.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a brush.
