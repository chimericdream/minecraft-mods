Wandering traders have a 20% chance of offering a **random pottery sherd**. It costs **8 emeralds and a brick**, and
the offer can be used once.

```item
id: angler_pottery_sherd
label: One random sherd
```

The sherd can be any of the 23 vanilla pottery sherds.

Suspicious blocks also hold sherds, see [Suspicious blocks](02-suspicious-blocks.md). Collecting every sherd earns the
**Preservationist** [advancement](06-advancements.md).
