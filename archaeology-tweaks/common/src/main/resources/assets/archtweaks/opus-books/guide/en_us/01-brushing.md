Every suspicious block in this mod works like vanilla suspicious sand and gravel.

1. Find a suspicious block.
2. Hold a brush and hold right-click on it.
3. The block slowly crumbles away, showing more of what is inside.
4. When it finishes, the loot pops out and the block turns into its normal version (suspicious clay becomes clay,
   suspicious soul sand becomes soul sand, and so on).

If you stop brushing partway, the block slowly goes back to looking untouched. Each finished block wears your brush
down by 1 durability.

## Tips

* Brushing a suspicious block for the first time earns the **First Dig** [advancement](06-advancements.md).
* Enchant your brush with [Gentle Touch](04-gentle-touch.md) for a chance at a bonus drop.
* Not sure where to look? See [Suspicious blocks](02-suspicious-blocks.md) for where each one generates.
