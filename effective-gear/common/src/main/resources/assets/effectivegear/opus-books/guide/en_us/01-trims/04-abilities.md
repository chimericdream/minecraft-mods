Two patterns give you an ability on top of their normal bonus. Both use the same key, **Use Trim Ability**, which
is **R** by default. You can change it in Controls, under the Effective Gear category.

You can only wear one full pattern set at a time, so one key covers whichever ability you currently have.

## Bolt

Wear a full set of Bolt-trimmed armor. While **sprinting on the ground**, press the ability key to burst forward.
There is a short cooldown (3 seconds) before you can dash again.

## Flow

Wear a full set of Flow-trimmed armor. While **in the air**, press the ability key to get a boost up and forward,
like a burst of wind underfoot. You can do this once per jump, with a short cooldown (1 second).

> [!NOTE]
> The key does nothing if the conditions are not met. Bolt needs you to be sprinting on the ground, and Flow needs
> you to be off the ground.
