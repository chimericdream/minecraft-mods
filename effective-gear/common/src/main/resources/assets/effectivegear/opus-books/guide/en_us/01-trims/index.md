Armor trims are no longer just for looks. Wear **all four pieces** (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots) with
the same trim, and you get a bonus for as long as you wear them.

There are three kinds of set bonus:

* [By material](02-material-bonuses.md): all four pieces trimmed with the same material, such as gold or
  emerald.
* [By pattern](03-pattern-bonuses.md): all four pieces trimmed with the same pattern, such as Wild or Eye. The
  material does not matter.
* [Netherite armor](02-material-bonuses.md#netherite-armor): a full set of netherite armor grants fire
  resistance.

Material and pattern bonuses stack with each other, so a full set can give you both.

Also in this chapter:

* [Pattern abilities](04-abilities.md): Bolt and Flow also give you an ability to trigger with a key.
* [New trim materials](05-new-materials.md): nine more things to trim with.

> [!NOTE] Armored Elytra
> If you use the Armored Elytra mod, an armored elytra counts as the chestplate for its trim and for the
> netherite armor bonus.
