Leaves normally change color to match the biome they are in. **Preserving** is an enchantment for shears. Leaves
you break with Preserving shears keep their original color forever, even when you place them somewhere with a
very different biome color. It is handy for building with one consistent leaf color.

## Getting it

Shears cannot be enchanted at an enchanting table, so apply Preserving with an **anvil** and a Preserving
enchanted book. The book can turn up in the enchanting table and in villager trades.

## Using it

Break a leaf block with the enchanted shears. The leaf you get shows **Color Preserved** in its tooltip. Place it
anywhere and it keeps its color.

It works on the leaves whose color changes by biome:

* Oak
* Jungle
* Acacia
* Dark Oak
* Mangrove

## Undoing it

Put a preserved leaf block alone in the crafting grid to turn it back into an ordinary one.

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [oak_leaves]
result: oak_leaves
```

> [!NOTE]
> The recipe is shown for oak leaves, but each of the five leaf types has its own.

Your first preserved leaf earns the [Colors that Pop](04-advancements.md) advancement.
