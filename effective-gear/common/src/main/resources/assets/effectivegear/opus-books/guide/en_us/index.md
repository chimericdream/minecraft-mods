Welcome to the Effective Gear guide. This mod adds small bonuses to the armor, tools, and weapons you already
use. Dress in a matching set of trimmed armor and you get a perk, with no extra items to carry.

## Where to start

* [Trim bonuses](01-trims/index.md): what a full set of trimmed armor does, and how the new trim materials work.
* [Preserving shears](02-preserving.md): keep leaves their original color wherever you place them.
* [Wandering traders](03-wandering-traders.md): a new way to find smithing templates.
* [Advancements](04-advancements.md): the goal this mod adds.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and an iron helmet.
