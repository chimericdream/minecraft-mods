# Effective Gear — Ideas

Feature ideas for this mod, from first thought to ready to build. The process is described in
[`docs/FEATURE-WORK.md`](../../docs/FEATURE-WORK.md).

## Identity

**Everything you wear and wield, made a little more interesting.** Tweaks and bonuses for armor, weapons, and
tools: trim materials with set bonuses today, and wielded-gear trims, enchantments, and other gear mechanics as
the mod grows. Bonuses start small and situational, and the rarer the material or the fuller the set, the more
they can matter. They never outclass vanilla's own progression.
