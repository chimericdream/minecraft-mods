# Blue ice trim material

> Source: the "Yes, but blue ice instead" vote on shortlist #5 (snow trim material), in
> [`brainstorms/2026-08-25-legacy/shortlist.md`](../brainstorms/2026-08-25-legacy/shortlist.md) · Effort **S**
> Status: **Exploring**

## Description

A blue ice trim material for cold-biome players. A full matching set grants a small bonus, following the
existing set-bonus pattern. The bonus itself is undecided. Snow's freezing immunity and powder-snow
walking fit blue ice poorly, so this idea needs its own.

## Decisions

- Blue ice is its own material and sits **alongside** the [snow trim material](snow-trim-material.md),
  not in place of it.

## Open questions
