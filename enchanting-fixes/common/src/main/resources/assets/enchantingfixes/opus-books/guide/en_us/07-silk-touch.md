The mod includes the "Enchanting Fixes: Advanced Silk Touch" data pack. It adds the Advanced Silk Touch
enchantment. The pack is off by default.

## Turn it on

* **New world:** turn on the pack in the Data Packs screen when you create the world.
* **Existing world:** use `/datapack enable`, then restart the world.

## What it collects

Each level collects the blocks of the lower levels, and these blocks:

| Level | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| I | Dirt paths and farmland |
| III | Suspicious sand and suspicious gravel, with their loot |
| V | Budding amethyst |
| VII | Infested blocks, with their silverfish |
| X | Spawners, with their mob |
| XV | Reinforced deepslate |
| XXX | Bedrock |

Advanced Silk Touch does not include the effect of Silk Touch. Put both on one tool to collect all these blocks.
It is incompatible with Fortune.

With Advanced Silk Touch XXX, you can break bedrock. It takes about 80 seconds.

## Where to get it

The enchanting table, librarians, loot, and mob equipment can all give it at low levels. A master librarian has a
small chance to sell a book at a level from X to XV. To get a higher level, combine two books or tools with the same
level in an anvil.

On a server, a player who is not an operator can place a spawner from Advanced Silk Touch X. The spawner gets only
the mob type, as with a spawn egg.

> [!WARNING]
> Bedrock is the floor of the Overworld and the Nether. Below the floor is the void. If you fall into the void, you
> lose your items.
