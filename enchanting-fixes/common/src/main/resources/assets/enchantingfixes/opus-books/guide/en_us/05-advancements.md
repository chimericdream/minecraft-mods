The mod adds advancements in a separate tab. With the default settings, you can earn Overpowered. To earn the others,
first raise the maximum levels in the [settings](04-settings.md).

| Advancement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| Overpowered | Get an enchantment at level X or higher. Enchanted books count. |
| But... why? | Get an enchantment at level C or higher. |
| That's a lotta loot(ing)! | Get a weapon with Looting L. |
| Scrooge McDuck would be proud. | Get a tool with Fortune L. |
| Deleted! | Hit a mob with a weapon that has Knockback XXXII or higher. |

Two more are hidden. One is for the highest level there is. The other is for the highest Knockback level.
