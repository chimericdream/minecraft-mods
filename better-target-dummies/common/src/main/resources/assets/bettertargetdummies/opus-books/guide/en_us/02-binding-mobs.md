"Binding" a mob means telling a dummy which mob it should show.

## Bind

Right-click a Target Dummy with a spawn egg.

* A real spawn egg is used up, as usual.
* A [Dummy Spawn Egg](03-dummy-spawn-egg.md) is not used up.

The action bar tells you which mob is bound and which way it faces. The mob is a real one, not a lookalike, so
effects such as Smite and Bane of Arthropods work on it normally.

To change the mob, right-click with a different egg at any time.

## Facing

The mob starts out facing the side of the dummy you were standing on when you bound it. Stand north of the dummy and
it faces north. Only the four main directions are used.

## Turn

Right-click the dummy with an empty hand to turn the mob 90 degrees clockwise. The action bar also shows what is
bound and which way it faces.

## Clear

Sneak and right-click the dummy with an empty hand. The mob and its binding are removed, and the action bar says
"Dummy cleared".

You can also break the block or use `/kill` on the mob to get rid of it.

> [!NOTE]
> Turning the power off removes the mob but keeps the binding. Clearing removes the binding as well.

| Action | What happens |
|:-------|:-------------|
| Right-click with a spawn egg | Binds that mob |
| Right-click with an empty hand | Turns the mob 90 degrees clockwise |
| Sneak and right-click with an empty hand | Clears the binding |
