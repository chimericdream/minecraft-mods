Welcome to the Better Target Dummies guide. This mod gives you a Target Dummy block that holds a real, motionless
mob, so you can test how your weapons, enchantments, and armor perform against it.

```item
id: bettertargetdummies:target_dummy
label: Target Dummy
```

## Where to start

* [The Target Dummy](01-target-dummy.md): crafting it, placing it, and powering it with redstone.
* [Binding a mob](02-binding-mobs.md): choosing which mob stands on the dummy, turning it, and clearing it.
* [Dummy Spawn Egg](03-dummy-spawn-egg.md): a reusable egg that lets you pick any mob from a list.
* [Testing damage](04-testing-damage.md): the damage readout and what the mob ignores.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a target block.
