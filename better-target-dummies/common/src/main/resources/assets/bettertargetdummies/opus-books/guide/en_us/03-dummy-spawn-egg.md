The Dummy Spawn Egg is a survival-friendly stand-in for a real spawn egg. You do not need to own a spawn egg for the
mob you want to test, and the Dummy Spawn Egg is never used up.

## Crafting

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [egg, paper]
result: bettertargetdummies:dummy_spawn_egg
```

It is also in the Combat tab of the creative inventory, and stacks to 16.

## Picking a mob

1. Hold the egg and right-click in the air.
2. A "Pick a Mob" list opens. Type in the search box to find the mob you want, then choose it.
3. Right-click a Target Dummy with the egg to bind that mob.

The egg's tooltip always shows which mob is picked. To pick a different mob, right-click in the air again. Sneak and
right-click in the air to forget the picked mob.

## Naming it instead

If you would rather type a mob's name, rename the egg in an anvil, for example to "Zombie" or "Cave Spider". Spaces
are fine. A picked mob takes priority over the name.

> [!TIP]
> If the egg has no mob yet, using it on a dummy just tells you to pick one or rename it.
