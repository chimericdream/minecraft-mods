You realised that if you flip a hopper upside-down, it moves items upward. What about gravity, you ask? Try not to think
about it too hard.

A **Hupper** is a hopper that pushes items **up** instead of down, which makes vertical item elevators easy. Huppers
come in the same speed tiers as hoppers, and as multi-huppers that send items up and out to several sides.

* [Hupper](01-hupper.md): the basic hupper and how to turn any hopper into one.
* [Multi-huppers](02-multi-huppers.md): huppers with several outputs.

> [!TIP]
> Use a [wrench](../06-wrench.md) to rotate a hupper after you place it.
