Not your granddaddy's item filter. Every Hopper X-Treme hopper and hupper, including the multi and glazed variants, can
use a filter. There is no separate "filtered" block to craft: just put a Hopper Item Filter into the hopper's filter
slot.

## Adding a filter to a hopper

Filter-ready hoppers have an extra slot on the right side of their screen. Put a filter in it to switch filtering on.
