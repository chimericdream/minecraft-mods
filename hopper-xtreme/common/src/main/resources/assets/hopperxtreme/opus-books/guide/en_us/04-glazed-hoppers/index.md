---
title: Glazed hoppers
icon: item:hopperxtreme:glazed_hopper
tags: [glazed]
summary: Hoppers with a smooth surface that don't hold on to items.
---
Thanks to the smooth surface of glazed terracotta, items don't stay in these hoppers for long. Anything in a glazed
hopper's inventory is ejected in the direction the hopper is facing, a bit like a dropper.

* A glazed hopper has room for just **one** stack, instead of five.
* Items come out as dropped items in front of the hopper, rather than being handed to a container.
* If the block in front of the hopper has a solid face, nothing comes out.
* Glazed hoppers keep the speed of the tier they came from.
