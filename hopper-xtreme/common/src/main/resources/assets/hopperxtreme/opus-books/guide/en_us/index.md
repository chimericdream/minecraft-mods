title: Hopper X-Treme
---
Welcome to the Hopper X-Treme guide! Vanilla hoppers are slow, only move items one way, and carry anything. This mod
gives you a whole family of hoppers that move items faster, send them to several places at once, push them
**upward**, and let you choose exactly what passes through.

## Where to start

* [Upgraded hoppers](01-upgraded-hoppers/index.md): the speed tiers, from the slow Honeyed Hopper to the Nether Star
  Hopper.
* [Multi-hoppers](02-multi-hoppers/index.md): one hopper that feeds up to five containers.
* [Huppers](03-huppers/index.md): hoppers that move items up instead of down.
* [Glazed hoppers](04-glazed-hoppers/index.md): hoppers that spit items out instead of holding them.
* [Item filters](05-item-filters/index.md): pick exactly which items a hopper accepts.
* [Wrench](06-wrench.md): rotate blocks and switch the sides of multi-hoppers.
* [Old filtered hoppers](07-old-filtered-hoppers.md): what happened to the "Filtered" blocks.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a hopper. A new world also hands you one the first time you join.
