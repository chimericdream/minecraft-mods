A Multi-Hopper can send items to as many as 5 containers at once: the four sides, plus the one below it. It takes turns
between them (round-robin), skipping any side that is switched off or has nothing to receive the items.

## Choosing the sides

Each side starts switched off. Right-click one of the hopper's arms with a [wrench](../06-wrench.md) to switch that side
on or off again.

## Tiers

Multi-hoppers come in the same speed tiers as regular hoppers, apart from the Honeyed and Copper ones.
