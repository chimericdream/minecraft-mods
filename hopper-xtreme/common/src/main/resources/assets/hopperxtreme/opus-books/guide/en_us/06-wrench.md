---
title: Wrench
icon: item:minecraft:iron_ingot
tags: [tools]
summary: Rotate blocks and switch multi-hopper sides.
---
The wrench is the ultimate multi-tool. Right-click a block with it to rotate things like glazed terracotta, redstone
components, and stairs, and to flip slabs. On hoppers it turns them to face a different way.

On a [multi-hopper](02-multi-hoppers/index.md#choosing-the-sides), multi-hupper, or glazed multi-hopper, right-click one
of the hopper's arms to switch that side on or off.

```recipe
pattern: [" # ", " ##", "#  "]
key:
  "#": iron_ingot
result: hopperxtreme:tools/wrench
```

> [!NOTE] Playing with Minekea?
> If Minekea is installed, it provides its own wrench and this mod does not add a second one. The Minekea wrench does
> the same jobs, including switching multi-hopper sides.
