Ever had trouble with hopper locking in a tight build? This hopper is for you. Thanks to copper's conductivity, it can
carry a redstone signal without blocking the transfer of items. In other words, a Copper Hopper can never be locked.

**Cooldown:** 8 game ticks (2.5 items per second, the same as a vanilla hopper)

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [hopper, copper_ingot]
result: hopperxtreme:copper_hopper
```
