The basic Multi-Hopper has the same speed as a vanilla hopper.

**Cooldown:** 8 game ticks (2.5 items per second)

Four hoppers make one Multi-Hopper:

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [hopper, hopper, hopper, hopper]
result: hopperxtreme:multi_hopper
```

Changed your mind? A Multi-Hopper breaks back down into four hoppers:

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [hopperxtreme:multi_hopper]
result: hopper
count: 4
```

> [!TIP]
> Remember to switch the sides on with a [wrench](../06-wrench.md). See [multi-hoppers](index.md#choosing-the-sides).
