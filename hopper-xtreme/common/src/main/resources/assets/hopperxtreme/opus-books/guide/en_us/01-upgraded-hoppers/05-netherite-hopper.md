The Netherite Hopper moves an item every single tick, eight times as fast as a vanilla hopper.

**Cooldown:** 1 game tick (20 items per second)

It is made at a smithing table, so you also need a netherite upgrade smithing template.

```recipe
type: smithing
template: netherite_upgrade_smithing_template
base: hopperxtreme:diamond_hopper
addition: netherite_ingot
result: hopperxtreme:netherite_hopper
```
