title: Old filtered hoppers
icon: item:hopperxtreme:hopper_item_filter
tags: [filters]
summary: What happened to the "Filtered" hopper blocks.
---
Earlier versions of Hopper X-Treme had separate "Filtered" hoppers, such as the Filtered Golden Hopper. Filtering is
now built into every hopper (see [item filters](05-item-filters/index.md)), so those blocks are **deprecated** and you
should not craft new ones.

If you still have one, it changes into its filter-ready equivalent when you place it, keeping its contents, custom
name, and filter settings.
