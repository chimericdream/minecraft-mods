Like hoppers, but better! Each upgraded hopper waits a different number of game ticks between transfers (there are 20
ticks in a second). A vanilla hopper waits 8.

| Hopper | Cooldown | Items per second |
|:-------|---------:|-----------------:|
| ![](item:hopperxtreme:honeyed_hopper) [Honeyed Hopper](01-honeyed-hopper.md) | 20 ticks | 1 |
| ![](item:hopperxtreme:copper_hopper) [Copper Hopper](02-copper-hopper.md) | 8 ticks | 2.5 |
| ![](item:hopperxtreme:golden_hopper) [Golden Hopper](03-golden-hopper.md) | 4 ticks | 5 |
| ![](item:hopperxtreme:diamond_hopper) [Diamond Hopper](04-diamond-hopper.md) | 2 ticks | 10 |
| ![](item:hopperxtreme:netherite_hopper) [Netherite Hopper](05-netherite-hopper.md) | 1 tick | 20 |
| ![](item:hopperxtreme:nether_star_hopper) [Nether Star Hopper](06-nether-star-hopper.md) | 1 tick | up to 320 |

Golden, Diamond, Netherite, and Nether Star hoppers build on each other: craft a Golden Hopper, upgrade it to Diamond,
then Netherite, then Nether Star.

Like a vanilla hopper, any of these (except the Copper Hopper) stops moving items while it is powered by redstone.

> [!TIP]
> Every hopper here also comes as a [multi-hopper](../02-multi-hoppers/index.md), a [hupper](../03-huppers/index.md),
> and a [glazed hopper](../04-glazed-hoppers/index.md), and every one of them works with an
> [item filter](../05-item-filters/index.md).
