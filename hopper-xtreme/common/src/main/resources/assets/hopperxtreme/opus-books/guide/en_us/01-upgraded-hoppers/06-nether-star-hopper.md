Beyond netherite! This is the ultimate hopper. It keeps the Netherite Hopper's 1-tick cooldown, but moves up to 16
items in a single transfer instead of just 1, never more than a full stack at a time.

**Cooldown:** 1 game tick (up to 320 items per second)
