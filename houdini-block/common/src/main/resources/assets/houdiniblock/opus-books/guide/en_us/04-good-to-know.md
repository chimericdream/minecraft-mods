## Drops

Breaking a Houdini Block in survival gives you exactly one back. It returns as a fresh block in the default mode,
**Prevent on break**, so switch it again if you need another mode. In creative mode nothing drops.

## Water

The block can be placed in water and sits waterlogged. When you break it, water is left behind in its spot.

## What it does not do

* It only quiets the block's own position. Breaking or placing other blocks next to it updates things as usual.
* Updates are only suppressed when the block goes in or out of empty space (air or water), or in Replace mode.
* It does not make anything unbreakable breakable.
