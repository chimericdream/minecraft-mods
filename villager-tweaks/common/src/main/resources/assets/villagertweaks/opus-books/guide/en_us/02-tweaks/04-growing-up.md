These settings are in the **Villager Growth** section.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|:--------|:-------------|:--------|
| Override grow up time | Use a fixed grow-up time for baby villagers. | Off |
| Villager grow up time | The fixed time, in ticks (20 ticks is one second). | 24000 |
| Display grow up time | Shows the time left until a baby villager grows up. | Off |

The default of 24000 ticks is 20 minutes, the same as vanilla. Change it to speed up (or slow down) your breeder.

The override covers both babies from spawn eggs and babies bred by villagers.

With **Display grow up time** on, the countdown (like `19:30`) appears as the baby's name. Its old name comes back
once it grows up.
