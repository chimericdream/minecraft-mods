The mod adds a **Villager Tweaks** tab with two advancements.

| Advancement | How to earn it |
|:------------|:---------------|
| Bag and Tag | Bag a villager who has reached max trading level. |
| Pied Piper | Lure 8 villagers to you at the same time. |

Pied Piper needs [Lure villagers](02-tweaks/05-luring-and-leashing.md) turned on.
