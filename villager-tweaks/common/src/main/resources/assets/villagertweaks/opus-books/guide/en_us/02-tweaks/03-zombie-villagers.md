These settings are in the **Zombie Conversion** section.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|:--------|:-------------|:--------|
| Zombies always convert villagers | A zombie that kills a villager always turns it into a zombie villager, whatever the difficulty. | Off |
| Override cure time | Use a fixed cure time instead of vanilla's random one. | Off |
| Villager cure time | The fixed time, in ticks (20 ticks is one second). | 3600 |
| Display cure time | Shows the time left until a zombie villager is cured. | Off |

## Always converting

This makes zombies treat every difficulty like Hard, so villagers are never simply killed. It is handy for setting up
a cure farm on a lower difficulty.

## Cure time

The default fixed time of 3600 ticks is three minutes. With **Display cure time** on, the countdown (like `2:45`)
appears as the zombie villager's name while it is being cured. Its old name comes back afterwards.
