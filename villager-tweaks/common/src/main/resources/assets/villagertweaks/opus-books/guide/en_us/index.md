Welcome to the Villager Tweaks guide. This mod smooths out the most common villager headaches: moving them around,
trading, curing, and breeding. Almost everything is a setting you can turn on or off.

```item
id: villagertweaks:bagged_villager
label: Bagged Villager
```

## Where to start

* [Bagged Villager](01-bagged-villager.md): pick up a villager in a bundle and put it down somewhere else.
* [The tweaks](02-tweaks/index.md): trading, reputation, zombie villagers, growing up, luring, and leashing nitwits.
* [Settings](03-settings.md): where to change the tweaks.
* [Advancements](04-advancements.md): two goals to work toward.

> [!TIP]
> Most tweaks are off until you enable them. Open the settings first (see [Settings](03-settings.md)).
