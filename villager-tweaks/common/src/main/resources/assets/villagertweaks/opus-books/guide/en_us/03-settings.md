Villager Tweaks has a settings screen with four sections: **Trading**, **Zombie Conversion**, **Villager Growth**, and
**Misc.**

* On Fabric, open it from the mod list with Mod Menu.
* The settings are saved in `villagertweaks.json5` in your game's `config` folder.

Every tweak is described on its page under [The tweaks](02-tweaks/index.md).

> [!TIP]
> Most tweaks are off by default, so a fresh install plays like vanilla apart from the Bagged Villager.
