Villagers remember how you treat them, and that changes their prices. These settings are in the **Trading** section.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|:--------|:-------------|:--------|
| Global reputation | Everyone in the world shares one reputation with each villager, instead of each player having their own. | Off |
| Negative reputation | Hitting or killing villagers lowers your reputation. Turn it off and those penalties no longer count. | On |

## Global reputation

With this on, one player curing a zombie villager gives every player the better prices. Trading, hurting, and killing
villagers also affect everyone.

## Turning off negative reputation

When **Negative reputation** is off, the penalties from hitting or killing villagers (and from
[bagging them](../01-bagged-villager.md)) are ignored when prices are worked out.
