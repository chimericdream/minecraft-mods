The Bagged Villager lets you move a villager without boats or minecarts. It keeps the villager's profession, level,
trades, and name.

```item
id: villagertweaks:bagged_villager
label: Bagged Villager
```

## Bagging a villager

Hold a **Bundle** and **sneak and right-click** a villager. The villager disappears and you get a Bagged Villager.

> [!WARNING] Empty the bundle first
> The bundle is used up when you bag a villager, so anything inside it is lost.

Bagging also counts as a small hit to the villager's reputation of you.

## Putting it down

* **On a block:** right-click the top of a block. There must be two blocks of open air above it.
* **In the air:** right-click while standing on the ground and the villager appears at your feet. It does nothing
  while you are jumping or flying.

If a spot doesn't work, the villager stays in the bag. When it is placed, you get a plain Bundle back.

## Seeing what's inside

Hover over a Bagged Villager to see its biome type, profession, and level, such as "Swamp Librarian · Level 4
(Expert)". Hold **Shift** to list what it sells. Only the items it sells are shown, not their prices, in the villager's
own order, followed by "…and N more" if the list is cut short.

![The tooltip of a bagged apprentice librarian, with a hint to hold Shift](texture:villagertweaks:guide/apprentice_tooltip){width=400}

![The tooltip of a bagged master librarian, with Shift held to list its trades](texture:villagertweaks:guide/master_tooltip){width=400}

How many trades are listed is set by **Bagged villager tooltip trades** in the **Misc.** settings (default 5). Set it to
0 to hide the list and the Shift hint.

## Good to know

* The Bagged Villager doesn't stack, and you can find it in the Functional Blocks creative tab.
* Bagging a villager who has reached the top trading level earns the [Bag and Tag](04-advancements.md) advancement.
