# Villager Tweaks — Ideas

Feature ideas for this mod, from first thought to ready to build. The process is described in
[`docs/FEATURE-WORK.md`](../../docs/FEATURE-WORK.md).

## Identity

> **Small, configurable fixes for real villager headaches** — trading halls, breeders, curing, and moving villagers around. Every idea below should stay a toggle, not a system.

## Active ideas

### Bagged villager

- [Bagged Villager tooltip](bagged-villager/bagged-villager-tooltip.md) — Show profession, level and trades on hover.

### Trading hall

- [Workstation checker](trading-hall/workstation-checker.md) — An item that shows why a villager won't take a job.
- [Restock rules config](trading-hall/restock-rules.md) — Toggles for how and when villagers restock.
- [Trade preview on locked trades](trading-hall/trade-preview.md) — See what later trades will be.

### Movement

- [Shackles](movement/shackles.md) — An item that keeps a villager from moving.

### Population

- [Breeding requirements config](population/breeding-requirements.md) — Adjust the bed and food requirements.
- [Panic toggle](population/panic-toggle.md) — Stop villagers panic-sprinting from zombies.

### Reputation

- [Reputation viewer](reputation/reputation-viewer.md) — Show your reputation with a villager.

### Progression

- ["Fresh Start" advancement](progression/fresh-start-advancement.md) — Cure and re-employ the same villager.
