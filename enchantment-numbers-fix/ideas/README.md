# Enchantment Numbers Fix — Ideas

Feature ideas for this mod, from first thought to ready to build. The process is described in
[`docs/FEATURE-WORK.md`](../../docs/FEATURE-WORK.md).

## Identity

> Simple mod that converts enchantment levels above 10 to Roman numerals instead of their decimal version.

## Active ideas

The legacy backlog was shortlisted in
[`brainstorms/2026-08-13-legacy/shortlist.md`](brainstorms/2026-08-13-legacy/shortlist.md). The gameplay
ideas that don't fit the identity are listed there under "Beyond the current identity" and are not active.

### Display

- [Fallback threshold](display/fallback-threshold.md) — Switch to decimal above a configurable level.
- [Numeral style option](display/numeral-style.md) — Choose extended Roman or decimal.
- [Over-vanilla highlighting](display/over-vanilla-highlighting.md) — Flag levels above the natural maximum.
- [Compact hybrid style](display/compact-hybrid-style.md) — Show both notations, like "X (10)".
- [Locale-respecting numerals](display/locale-numerals.md) — Per-language numeral overrides.

### Effects

- [Potion and status effect amplifiers](effects/effect-amplifiers.md) — Roman numerals for effect amplifiers too.

### Tooltips

- [Configurable tooltip sorting](tooltips/tooltip-sorting.md) — Choose how enchantments are ordered.
- [Configurable tooltip colors and icons](tooltips/tooltip-colors-and-icons.md) — Customize enchantment lines.
