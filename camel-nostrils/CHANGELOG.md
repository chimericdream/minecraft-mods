### Unreleased changes

#### New Features

* Leads can now be attached to fish (cod, salmon, pufferfish, tropical fish). It doesn't let them live outside water, though.
* Added the Livna block: an anvil that falls *upward* instead of down, damaging anything unlucky enough to be above it. It works just like a regular anvil otherwise, including taking damage over repeated uses (turning into a Chipped Livna, then a Damaged Livna).
* Leashing a camel and letting it fall too far behind now has a small chance of ripping its snout clean off. The snout drops as an item, and pulling one off for the first time unlocks a hidden advancement.
* Added three more advancements: leashing a fish for the first time, a leashed fish dying from being out of water, and placing a Livna block for the first time.
