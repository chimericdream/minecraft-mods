The Camel Nostrils tab has these goals. Some are hidden until you earn them.

| Advancement | How |
|:------------|:----|
| Fish Walker | Put a lead on a fish. |
| Fish Out of Water | Let a leashed fish die out of water. |
| Faster Than a Snail | Take damage from a zombie fish. |
| Camel Nostrils?!? | Pull a camel's snout off. |
| Miracle Cure | Grow a camel's snout back. |
| Miracle-Gro | Grow up a baby chicken or camel with golden food. |
| It could be full of chocolate okay | Eat a Golden Egg. |
| What Goes Up... | Place a livna. |
| Nap Time | Sleep in an upside-down bed. |
