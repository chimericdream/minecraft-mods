---
title: Zombie fish
icon: item:minecraft:rotten_flesh
tags: [fish, mobs]
summary: A leashed salmon, cod, or tropical fish that dies out of water comes back hostile.
---
If a leashed salmon, cod, or tropical fish dies from being out of water while you are holding its lead, it
doesn't stay dead. It comes back as a zombie fish, and you're still holding the lead.

```entity
id: camelnostrils:zombie_salmon
scale: 1.5
```

```entity
id: camelnostrils:zombie_cod
scale: 1.5
```

```entity
id: camelnostrils:zombie_tropical_fish
scale: 1.5
```

* It has only 1 heart of health.
* Sunlight doesn't hurt it.
* It still just flops around, but now it flops toward you.
* Its tail-slap does a little damage and poisons you for about 4 seconds.

> [!WARNING] Poison
> The poison is short, but the slap comes quickly. Drop the lead and deal with the fish.
