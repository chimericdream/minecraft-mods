---
title: Pulling a snout off
icon: item:minecraft:lead
tags: [camel]
summary: Lead a camel too far and it may lose its snout.
---
Put a lead on a camel and walk away. If the camel lags too far behind and the lead stretches until it lets go,
there is a small chance (5%) that the snout comes off with it.

```entity
id: camel
scale: 1
```

* The snout drops as a head item named "Camel Snout".
* The camel carries on without it, and the lead is dropped either way.
* Baby camels are safe. This only happens to adult camels.
* A camel that has already lost its snout can't lose it again.

> [!TIP]
> A camel without a snout isn't a lost cause. See [Growing a snout back](02-regrowing.md).
