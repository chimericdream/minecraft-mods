Welcome to the Camel Nostrils guide. This mod is absurd on purpose: every feature started life as a suggestion
from someone who doesn't play Minecraft. Expect ripped-off camel snouts, hostile zombie fish, golden food, and
blocks hanging upside down.

## Where to start

* [Camels](01-camels/index.md): what happens when a leashed camel lags behind, and how to fix it.
* [Fish](02-fish/index.md): leashing fish, and what happens when one dies out of water.
* [Golden foods](03-golden-foods/index.md): golden wheat seeds, golden eggs, and the golden cactus.
* [Upside-down blocks](04-upside-down/index.md): the livna, beds, crafting table, and chest.
* [Advancements](05-advancements.md): a set of goals to work through.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a cactus.
