---
title: Walking a fish
icon: item:minecraft:lead
tags: [fish]
summary: Leads work on fish. That's about all you get.
---
You can attach a lead to a fish, just like any other animal. It doesn't make them live outside water, though.

Take a leashed fish out of the water and it will flop around and eventually die. Read on to see what comes of that
for salmon, cod, and tropical fish: [Zombie fish](02-zombie-fish.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Other fish, such as pufferfish, just die normally.
