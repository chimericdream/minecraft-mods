Feed a [Golden Cactus](../03-golden-foods/03-golden-cactus.md) to an adult camel that has lost its snout and the
snout grows back.

```item
id: camelnostrils:golden_cactus
```

Feed one to a baby camel instead and it grows up right away. A golden cactus does nothing for an adult camel that
still has its snout.
