Upside-down beds hang from the ceiling and come in all sixteen dye colors.

Craft one like a regular bed, with the planks and wool swapped. Any planks work:

```recipe
pattern: ["PPP", "WWW"]
key:
  P: [oak_planks, spruce_planks, birch_planks]
  W: red_wool
result: camelnostrils:red_upside_down_bed
```

Use the wool color you want: the same recipe works for every color.

* Sleep in one, even during the day, and it turns straight to night.
* There has to be room below the bed to lie down, and a solid block above to hold it up.
* It doesn't set your respawn point.
