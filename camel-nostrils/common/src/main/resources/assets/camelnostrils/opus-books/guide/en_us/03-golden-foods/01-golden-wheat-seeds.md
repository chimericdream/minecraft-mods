Eight wheat seeds around a gold ingot make 8 golden wheat seeds.

```recipe
pattern: ["WWW", "WGW", "WWW"]
key:
  W: wheat_seeds
  G: gold_ingot
result: camelnostrils:golden_wheat_seeds
count: 8
```

Feed one to a chicken:

* **Adult chicken:** a 15% chance it lays a [Golden Egg](02-golden-egg.md).
* **Baby chicken:** it grows up right away.
