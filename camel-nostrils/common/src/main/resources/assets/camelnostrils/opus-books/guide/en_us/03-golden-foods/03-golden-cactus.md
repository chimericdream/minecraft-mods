Eight cacti around a gold ingot make 8 golden cacti.

```recipe
pattern: ["CCC", "CGC", "CCC"]
key:
  C: cactus
  G: gold_ingot
result: camelnostrils:golden_cactus
count: 8
```

It is placed and grows just like a regular cactus. Feed it to a camel:

* **Adult camel without a snout:** the [snout grows back](../01-camels/02-regrowing.md).
* **Baby camel:** it grows up right away.
