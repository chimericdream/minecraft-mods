Sneak off the edge of a block without looking down, and you keep walking at the same height, as though there were
solid ground under you. It works across a gap of up to 3 blocks by default.

## How to use

1. Hold the sneak key and walk toward an edge, looking ahead or up.
2. Keep going. You glide across the gap.
3. Reach the other side and you land normally.

## When you fall

You drop as usual if any of these happen:

* You look down. The default angle is 30 degrees below straight ahead.
* The gap is wider than the limit.
* You let go of the sneak key while in the air. You cannot bridge again until you land.

It does not apply while flying in creative, swimming, riding something, gliding with elytra, or in spectator mode.

> [!TIP]
> The gap size and look-down angle can be changed in the [settings](../02-settings.md).

You earn *Who Needs Bridges?* the first time you cross a gap, and a hidden advancement for looking down mid-way.
