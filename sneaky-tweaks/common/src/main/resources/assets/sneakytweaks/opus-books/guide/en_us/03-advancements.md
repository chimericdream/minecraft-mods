The mod adds a small set of advancements in its own tab.

| Advancement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| Watch Your Step | Sneak through a sweet berry bush without taking damage. |
| Cold Feet | Sneak on a lit campfire without taking damage. |
| Who Needs Bridges? | Cross a gap while sneaking without looking down. |

Two more are hidden, so keep experimenting. One has to do with overstaying your welcome at a fire, and the other
with gravity.
