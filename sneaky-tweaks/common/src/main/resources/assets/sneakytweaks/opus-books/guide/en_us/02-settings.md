---
title: Settings
icon: item:minecraft:comparator
tags: [settings]
summary: Turn features on or off and tune them.
---
Open the settings from the mods list. They are saved in a file
called `sneaky-tweaks.json5` in your config folder.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enable Timed Campfire Immunity | On | Turns the campfire feature on or off. |
| Campfire Grace Duration (ticks) | 200 | How long the meter lasts. There are 20 ticks in a second, so 200 is 10 seconds. |
| Enable Crouch Bridging | On | Turns crouch bridging on or off. |
| Crouch Bridge Max Gap (blocks) | 3 | The widest gap you can cross. Minimum 1. |
| Crouch Bridge Look-Down Threshold (degrees) | 30 | How far down you can look before you fall. From 1 to 89. |

Sweet berry bushes have no setting.
