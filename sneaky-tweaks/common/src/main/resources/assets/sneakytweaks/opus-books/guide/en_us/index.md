title: Sneaky Tweaks
---
Welcome to the Sneaky Tweaks guide. This mod adds no new blocks or items. It just makes a few things in the game
behave differently while you are sneaking.

## Where to start

* [Sweet berry bushes](01-features/01-berry-bushes.md): sneak through them without a scratch.
* [Campfire immunity](01-features/02-campfires.md): stand on a lit campfire for a little while.
* [Crouch bridging](01-features/03-crouch-bridging.md): walk across small gaps without falling.
* [Settings](02-settings.md): turn features on or off and tune them.
* [Advancements](03-advancements.md): a short set of goals.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a pair of leather boots.
