While you sneak on a lit campfire or soul campfire, it does not hurt you. The protection is not unlimited. A row
of flame icons next to your air bubbles shows how much you have left, and it shrinks the longer you stay put.

## How it works

* The meter starts full, lasts about 10 seconds by default, and drains while you sneak on a lit campfire.
* A soul campfire drains it twice as fast as a regular one, just as it hurts twice as much in vanilla.
* When the meter is empty, the fire hurts you again.
* Step off, or stop sneaking, and the meter refills.
* An unlit campfire is not a danger, so nothing changes there.

> [!TIP]
> The length of the meter can be changed in the [settings](../02-settings.md).

You earn *Cold Feet* for sneaking on a campfire, and a hidden advancement if you stay until the meter runs out.
