```item
minecraft:sweet_berries
```

Normally a sweet berry bush pricks you when you brush against it. While you are sneaking, it does nothing to you
at all.

## How to use

Hold the sneak key and walk through the bush. Let go of it and the bush pricks you as usual.

> [!NOTE]
> This one has no setting. It is always on.

You earn the *Watch Your Step* advancement the first time you do it.
