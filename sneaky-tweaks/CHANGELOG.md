### 26.2 - 1.0.0

#### Initial release!

* Sneaking through a sweet berry bush no longer damages you.
* Sneaking on a lit campfire or soul campfire grants temporary damage immunity via a depleting grace
  meter, shown as a row of flame icons next to the air-bubble meter. It runs out after a few seconds
  of continuous sneaking and refills once you step off. Duration and an on/off toggle are
  configurable.
* Added advancements: sneaking through a berry bush unscathed, sneaking on a campfire, and a hidden
  advancement for outlasting the campfire grace meter.
