Put in 3 apples, a glass bottle, and some fuel. After a short press you get a bottle of apple cider and a piece of
pomace. Charcoal or coal gives 8 pressings, and blaze powder gives 20. Hoppers can feed the press and empty it.

## Drinks

* **Apple Cider** restores hunger and gives you the empty bottle back.
* **Sweet Berry Juice** and **Glow Berry Juice**: press 3 sweet berries or 3 glow berries instead of apples. They
  drink the same as apple cider.

## Pomace

The leftover pomace can be composted, used as fertilizer, and fed to pigs to breed them. Feeding it to a hurt pig,
cow, sheep, or horse heals them a little.
