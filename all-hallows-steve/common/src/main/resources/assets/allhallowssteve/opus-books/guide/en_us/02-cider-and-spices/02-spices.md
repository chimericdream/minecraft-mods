There are three spices, and together they make mulling spices.

| Spice | How to get it |
|:------|:--------------|
| **Cinnamon** | Sometimes drops when you strip a cherry, mangrove, or jungle log with an axe. |
| **Cloves** | Smelt a small flower (dandelion, poppy, azure bluet, cornflower, oxeye daisy, and more) in a furnace or smoker. |
| **Nutmeg** | Smelt cocoa beans in a furnace or smoker, or buy it from a Journeyman farmer: 1 emerald for 2 nutmeg. |

## Mulling spices

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [allhallowssteve:cinnamon, allhallowssteve:cloves, allhallowssteve:nutmeg]
result: allhallowssteve:mulling_spices
```
