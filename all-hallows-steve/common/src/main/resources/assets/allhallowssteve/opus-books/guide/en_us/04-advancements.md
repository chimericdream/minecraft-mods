The mod adds a set of advancements in its own tab. They cover crafting the station, dyeing, carving, wearing
pumpkins, lighting them in new ways, and collecting stencils.

A second branch covers the Cider Press, pomace, spices, juices, and mulled drinks. Drink every kind of mulled drink
to earn the secret *Season's Drinkings* and some experience.

Three advancements are secret, so keep experimenting.
