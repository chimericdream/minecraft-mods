title: Mulled drinks

1. Pour 3 bottles of apple cider into a cauldron.
2. Right-click the cauldron with mulling spices. You only need them once per batch.
3. Set the cauldron over a lit campfire or soul campfire.
4. After about 30 seconds it turns into mulled apple cider.
5. Use empty glass bottles to ladle out 3 servings.

> [!NOTE]
> The cauldron won't start simmering until the spices are in. If the fire goes out, the simmer starts over but the
> spices stay.

Sweet berry juice and glow berry juice mull exactly the same way and make **Mulled Sweet Berry Juice** and
**Mulled Glow Berry Juice**. Each juice needs its own cauldron, and different juices can't be mixed.

Every serving restores 8 hunger.
