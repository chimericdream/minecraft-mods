Decorated pumpkins can be worn in the helmet slot. Lit ones can't.

![A player wearing a dyed, carved pumpkin, with lit pumpkins beside them](texture:allhallowssteve:gui/guide/worn_third_person){width=400}

* Other players see your dyed, carved pumpkin, and endermen won't be angered when you look at them.
* In first person you see through the carved openings. An uncarved face blocks your view completely.
* Choose which side faces forward with the "Turn Worn Pumpkin" key. It's unbound by default, so set one in Controls.
* A config option (also in Mod Menu) lowers how much the worn pumpkin blocks your view.
