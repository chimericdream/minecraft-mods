The Pumpkin Carving Station dyes and carves pumpkins.

```recipe
pattern: ["IS", "PC", "PP"]
key:
  I: iron_ingot
  S: shears
  C: crafting_table
  P: [oak_planks, spruce_planks, birch_planks]
result: allhallowssteve:carving_station
```

## Dyeing

Place a pumpkin and up to 3 dyes in the station to get a coloured pumpkin. Dyed pumpkins can be dyed again, and new
dyes mix with the colour already there.

![Dyeing a pumpkin green](texture:allhallowssteve:gui/guide/carving_station_dyeing)

![Pumpkins in every colour](texture:allhallowssteve:gui/guide/rainbow){width=400}

## Carving

Place up to four stencils around the pumpkin to carve its four sides. Stencils aren't used up, so you can reuse
them. The finished pumpkin spins in the output slot so you can see every side.

![Carving all four sides](texture:allhallowssteve:gui/guide/carving_station_carving)

![A pumpkin carved on each side](texture:allhallowssteve:gui/guide/four_sides){width=400}

## Inspecting

Sneak with an empty hand on a decorated pumpkin to see what's inside it.
