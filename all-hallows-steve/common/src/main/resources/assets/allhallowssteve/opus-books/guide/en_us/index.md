Welcome to the All Hallows Steve guide. This mod is all about autumn: carving and dyeing pumpkins, wearing them,
pressing cider, and baking spooky treats.

![Carved pumpkins in a row](texture:allhallowssteve:gui/guide/carved_pumpkins){width=400}

## Where to start

* [Pumpkins](01-pumpkins/index.md): the Pumpkin Carving Station, stencils, lighting, and wearing your work.
* [Cider and spices](02-cider-and-spices/index.md): the Cider Press, spices, and mulled drinks.
* [Harvest foods](03-harvest-foods.md): soul cakes, soup, and pies.
* [Advancements](04-advancements.md): a set of goals to work through.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a pumpkin.
