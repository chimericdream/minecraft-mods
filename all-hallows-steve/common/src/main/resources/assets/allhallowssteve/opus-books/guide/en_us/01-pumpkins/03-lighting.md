## Torches

Craft a decorated pumpkin with a torch to light it. It keeps its colour and carving. Each torch gives a different
glow:

| Torch | Light | Glow |
|:------|:------|:-----|
| Torch | 15 | Orange |
| Soul Torch | 10 | Blue |
| Copper Torch | 14 | Green |
| Redstone Torch | 7 | Red |

Shear a lit pumpkin to get the torch back.

![Pumpkins lit with a torch, a soul torch, a copper torch, and a redstone torch, left to right](texture:allhallowssteve:gui/guide/lit_variants){width=400}

## Candles

Sneak and use a candle on a decorated pumpkin, then add up to four. Each candle adds light (3, 6, 9, 12).

* Snuff them with an empty hand.
* Relight them with flint and steel.
* Shear the pumpkin to take them back out.

> [!NOTE]
> A pumpkin holds either candles or a torch, not both.

![An unlit pumpkin, then one to four candles, then a torch: the glow grows with each light](texture:allhallowssteve:gui/guide/candle_lighting){width=400}

## Plain pumpkins and dispensers

Use a torch on a carved pumpkin to make a jack o'lantern, or shears on a jack o'lantern to take the torch back out.
Dispensers can light and unlight pumpkins (torches and candles) and shear out their contents.
