Here are some feature ideas organized by theme, all aimed at a warm, atmospheric autumn/halloween vibe rather than silly or scary:

## Atmosphere & Ambiance

- **Ground fog** — a particle effect that rolls low across certain biomes on autumn nights, denser near water and forests
- **Misty morning weather cycle** — a softer fog weather type (lighter than vanilla fog) that lingers until midday
- **Wind chimes** — hanging blocks that emit subtle, randomized note sounds when the player walks near them or when wind is active
- **Harvest moon event** — a rare night where the moon has a warm amber tint and mobs spawn with a subtle orange glow effect
- **Falling leaves particles** — leaf blocks that periodically shed colored particles in autumn, without needing to break

## Harvest & Agriculture

- **Corn stalks** — tall, growable crops that drop corn (food) and can be woven into corn husk decorations; great for maze-building
- **Apple trees** — new tree variant that drops apples on its own in autumn; apples can be pressed into cider
- **Gourds** — additional pumpkin-family crops (white, striped, warty variants) grown from new seeds, decorative-only
- **Hay bale textures** — vanilla hay bales but with seasonal variants (fresh-cut, weathered)
- **Acorn drops** — oak trees shed acorns in autumn that grow into small oak saplings faster than normal

## Cuisine & Brewing

- **Cider press block** — multi-block structure that turns apples into apple cider (drinkable, slight warmth effect)
- **Mulled wine / spiced cider** — brewed potions with cozy effects: removes mining fatigue, slight saturation, brief frost resistance
- **Pumpkin pie & apple pie** — food items with high saturation, baked in a furnace with new recipes
- **Spice rack items** — cinnamon, nutmeg, clove used as ingredients for the above
- **Roasted chestnuts** — small snack food with minor regeneration

## Wildlife & Nature

- **Crows** — passive ambient mob that perches on fences and posts, occasionally flies between them with cawing sounds
- **Squirrels** — small neutral mob that darts around oak trees, drops acorns, can be fed to become trusting (follows you, leads to nuts)
- **Migratory butterflies** — decorative mob that drifts through flower-heavy biomes in autumn colors (orange, rust, gold)
- **Foraging mushrooms** — new mushroom variants (chanterelle, porcini shapes) that spawn only in autumn and are edible/cooking ingredients

## Decoration & Building

- **Wreaths** — placeable on doors, craftable in seasonal variants (autumn leaves, pinecones, wheat sheaves)
- **Pumpkin lanterns** — when you light your carved pumpkin with flint and steel, it becomes a custom-designed jack-o'-lantern light source with the brightness scaling to dye color
- **Corn husk bundles** — stackable decorative block with a distinct texture
- **Wheat sheaves** — bundled wheat stalks, placeable upright for field aesthetics
- **Gnarled branches & driftwood** — new log/wood variants with twisting textures, great for spooky-but-not-scenery builds
- **Iron lanterns** — vanilla-style lanterns but in black iron with amber glass, fitting for autumn lighting

## Wearables & Customization

- **Knit caps & scarves** — cosmetic head/hand wearables in autumn colors (rust, mustard, forest green)
- **Quilted capes** — decorative back-slot item (or cosmetic chestplate override) with patchwork textures
- **Wool cloaks** — dyeable cape-style wearables
- **Carved pumpkin variations on armor stands** — armor stands displaying your carved pumpkins for in-home decoration

## Worldgen & Exploration

- **Autumn forest biome** — a new biome variant with persistent orange/red foliage, denser fog, and exclusive mob spawns (crows, squirrels, foraging mushrooms)
- **Abandoned orchard** — a rare structure: weathered barn, scattered apple trees, and a hidden cellar with loot
- **Pumpkin patches** — naturally spawning clusters of mixed gourds in plains-adjacent biomes during autumn

A unifying mechanic that ties several of these together would be a **seasonal calendar** that triggers fall-specific worldgen, mob spawns, and leaf colors — that way the mod feels like the world itself is changing, not just that you've placed some new blocks.
