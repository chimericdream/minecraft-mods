Here are a dozen autumn-themed (but not “campy” or overtly horror) ideas that could slot neatly into your pumpkin-carving mod and expand its seasonal flavor:

1. Pumpkin Cider Press  
   • Craft a small “cider press” block. Whole pumpkins plus a little sugar yield pumpkin cider, which gives a mild “warmth” buff (slightly faster stamina regen) when sipped.

2. Harvest Scarecrow  
   • A decorative yet functional scarecrow you assemble from sticks, hay bales, and carved pumpkin head. Within a 10-block radius, it reduces crop trampling and deters crows (ambient noise & fewer flying crow mobs).

3. Seasonal Leaf Piles  
   • Breakable “leaf pile” blocks that spawn naturally under oak/maple trees. Walking through them kicks up colored‐leaf particles and slightly slows sprinting (playful crunch effect).

4. Gourds & Winter Squash  
   • New pumpkin relatives (e.g. butternut, acorn squash) that you plant from seeds. They can be carved or simply roasted on a campfire for a minor hunger and saturation boost.

5. Lantern Variants with Redstone Integration  
   • “Carved Lanterns” that you can wire into redstone circuits. Different stencil shapes produce different light-levels or colored light (using dyes) for atmospheric lighting designs.

6. Autumn Breeze Particles  
   • A client‐side particle effect you can toggle on/off: swirling orange and brown leaf particles whenever the wind “state” is active. Automatically turns on in biomes during pumpkin season.

7. Orchard Trees & Press  
   • Add apple/maple trees that you can graft saplings onto existing oaks. Their fruit can be pressed (using the same cider press) into apple cider or maple syrup.

8. Scents & Candles  
   • A “spice candle” you craft by combining pumpkin carving shavings plus cinnamon (from a new spice bush). When lit, it gives a small “comfort” status effect (reduces monster‐spawn radius by a tiny amount).

9. Festival NPC Villagers  
   • A wandering “harvest merchant” in seasonal attire who trades specialty dyes, rare carving stencils, cider, gourds, or festive banner patterns—only appears when your world’s calendar hits early October.

10. Dried Gourds and Wreaths  
    • A simple crafting chain: carve and hang gourds or dried leaves on walls to make autumn wreaths (purely decorative) and seasonal garlands you can drape over fences.

11. Hayride Cart  
    • A special minecart variant with hay‐bale seats. When two players hop in and a powered rail is activated, ambient harvest music plays, and the cart gives passengers a mild “joy” effect (slightly faster XP gain).

12. Pumpkin Lantern Alignment Ritual  
    • A small four-lantern ritual: arrange carved lanterns in a circle and activate (with a flint & steel). It grants a temporary area buff—like improved night vision and reduced fall damage—for villagers or players standing inside.

Each of these ideas builds on the existing pumpkin/carving theme, leans into the cozy side of autumn, and avoids silly “trick” gamification or outright horror. Let me know if you’d like more detail on crafting recipes, mechanics tuning, or even mock-ups of UI or block models!
