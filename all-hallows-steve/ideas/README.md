# All Hallows Steve — Ideas

Feature ideas for this mod, from first thought to ready to build. The process is described in
[`docs/FEATURE-WORK.md`](../../docs/FEATURE-WORK.md). Raw brainstorming sessions are kept in
[`brainstorms/`](brainstorms/), one dated folder per session.

## Identity

> An autumn/harvest/festival mod. Started with dyeing, carving, lighting, and displaying decorated pumpkins.
> The tone is grounded autumn: quiet, seasonal, and rooted in real harvest and Samhain folklore. Minimally
> campy, not horror, and not a general seasons or farming mod.

## Active ideas

### Carving

- [More stencils](carving/more-stencils.md) — Autumn and folklore motifs (oak leaf, owl, raven, moon
  phases) plus eerie vanilla designs such as the warden and sculk shrieker.
- [Stencils as exploration loot and trades](carving/stencil-loot-and-trades.md) — Rarer designs found in
  structures and sold by specific villager professions.
- [Carving yields seeds](carving/carving-seeds.md) — Carving returns pumpkin seeds (roasting them is in
  Harvest foods).
- [Pumpkin aging + honeycomb waxing](carving/pumpkin-aging-and-waxing.md) — Placed pumpkins slowly soften
  through a few stages; honeycomb locks the current stage, like copper.

### Crops

- [White pumpkins and gourd varieties](crops/white-pumpkins-and-gourds.md) — A few new varieties to start:
  white pumpkins for truer dye colors, warty heirlooms, and ornamental gourds.
- [Tall corn](crops/tall-corn.md) — A 2–3 block tall crop for field mazes, with husks for corn shocks.

### Harvest crafts

- [Harvest bundles and wreaths](harvest-crafts/harvest-bundles-and-wreaths.md) — Decorative corn shocks,
  wheat sheaves, dried-flower and herb bundles, and door wreaths.
- [Drying rack](harvest-crafts/drying-rack.md) — Visibly dries herbs, flowers, and apples over time into
  bundles and dried food.
- [Turnip lanterns](harvest-crafts/turnip-lanterns.md) — A small hanging carved-root lantern, after the
  Irish and Scottish tradition that came before pumpkins.

### Farm creatures

- [Scarecrow](farm-creatures/scarecrow.md) — A dressable figure with a pumpkin head that stops crop
  trampling and rabbits eating crops nearby.
- [Crows](farm-creatures/crows.md) — Ambient birds that perch, peck at crops, and scatter as a flock;
  scarecrows keep them away.

### Folklore

- [Weathering gravestones + iron fencing](folklore/gravestones-and-iron-fencing.md) — Writable
  headstones that gather moss over time and can be waxed, plus wrought-iron fencing and gates.
- [Will-o'-the-wisps + buried caches](folklore/will-o-the-wisps.md) — Faint lights in swamps and Pale
  Gardens that sometimes lead to buried caches holding the rarest stencils.

## Inbox

- Spice dyes: an expanded autumn dye palette (burnt sienna, ochre, russet, amber) using spices; see
  [Spices](harvest-crafts/spices.md) and brainstorm "Expanded autumn dye palette" [9, 10].
- Spice candles: scented candles with a mild "comfort" effect, such as slightly reduced monster spawning
  nearby; brainstorm "Spice candles" [5].
- A more complex, distinctive model for the cider press (e.g. a wooden screw press), as a later upgrade.
