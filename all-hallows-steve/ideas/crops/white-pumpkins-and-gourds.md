# White pumpkins and gourd varieties

> Shortlist #13 · Tier 2 — Solid mid-size features · Effort **M** · Value ★★★ · Votes 10 · My vote **Maybe**

## Description

The most-requested idea overall. Start with two or three varieties rather than ten:
- **White pumpkin**: a clean base that makes dyed colors look truer. It plugs straight into the station.
- **Warty/heirloom pumpkin**: a different texture, and still carvable.
- **Ornamental gourds**: small non-full blocks for piles and tabletop displays.

Each one needs a stem/crop, a block, seeds, and a way to find them (rare seeds from grass or loot). Add
more varieties only once the pipeline exists.

## Brainstorm variants

From [combined-ideas § 4](../brainstorms/2026-09-29/combined-ideas.md#4-gourds-crops--farming) and
[§ 1](../brainstorms/2026-09-29/combined-ideas.md#1-carving-station--decorated-pumpkins). Bracketed numbers are the
`brainstorms/2026-09-29/agentN.md` files that suggested each variant.

- White "ghost" pumpkins, warty heirlooms, striped gourds, butternut, acorn, delicata, blue Hubbard,
  Cushaw, Jarrahdale, turban squash, and small ornamental gourds [1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13].
  - White pumpkins make great bases for dyeing [13].
  - Each variety serves a purpose (baking, soup, seeds, decoration) so they aren't inventory clutter [2].
  - Some carvable, some decorative, some edible [1, 5, 9].
  - Different rind hardness, carving detail, and light transmission, so some make better lanterns and
    some are better to eat [7]. Different shapes get different stencil sets [8].
  - Found as rare seeds [13]. Can be stacked into piles or cornucopias [10].
- **Carving other gourds** [9, 13] — Small gourds carve into tiny hanging lights or gourd "bells".
- **Pumpkin patches** [9, 11] — Natural clusters of pumpkins and gourds in plains biomes.
- **Giant pumpkins** [1] — A slow, carefully tended crop that grows into a 2×2 or 3×3 centerpiece. (An
  honorable mention in the shortlist.)
