# Crows

> Shortlist #23 · Tier 3 — Big bets · Effort **L** · Value ★★ · Votes 6 · My vote **Yes**

## Description

Ambient birds that perch on fences, peck at crops, and scatter as a flock when you approach. On their
own they're just atmosphere, but together with the scarecrow (#16) they form a small gameplay loop. A
full new mob (model, animations, AI, spawning, sounds) is the most expensive part.

## Brainstorm variants

From [combined-ideas § 9](../brainstorms/2026-09-29/combined-ideas.md#9-creatures). Bracketed numbers are the
`brainstorms/2026-09-29/agentN.md` files that suggested each variant.

- **Crows or ravens** [1, 5, 10, 11, 12, 13] — Passive birds that perch on fences and scarecrows, peck at
  crops, and scatter as a flock when approached. They tie into the scarecrow.
- **Migrating birds** [7, 8, 10] — Geese or crows flying overhead in V formations. Ambient only. (Cut from
  the shortlist as scope creep.)
- The Thin Night event [13] would bring more crows. (Shortlist #24, voted **No**.)
