## Identity

**Ultraviolet light, a second kind of light in Minecraft.** UV light is the foundation: blocks that emit it,
ways to imbue other blocks with it, gear that reveals it, and the effects it has, like keeping mobs from
spawning without lighting up the area. Anything UV-themed fits, from hidden markings and glowing paint to
fluorescent blocks and mobs, along with complementary ideas that come up later. It starts vanilla-friendly and
additive, with existing spawning rules unchanged unless a player opts in, and it's open to a more ambitious
mechanic when the idea is right.
