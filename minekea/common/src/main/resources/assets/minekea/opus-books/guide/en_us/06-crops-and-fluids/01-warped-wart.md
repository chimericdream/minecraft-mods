What do you expect? It is like nether wart, but less red. Plant it on soul sand. Warped wart does not generate naturally.

Nine warped wart make a warped wart block, which can be turned back into nine warped wart.

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [warped_wart_block]
result: minekea:crops/warped_wart
count: 9
```

![A patch of warped wart](texture:minekea:gui/patchouli/warped_wart_patch){width=200}

Use it to make [warped nether bricks](../01-building-blocks/07-warped-nether-bricks.md).
