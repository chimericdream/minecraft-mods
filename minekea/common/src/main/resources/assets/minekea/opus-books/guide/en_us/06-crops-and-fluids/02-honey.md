Honey is a thick, slow-flowing fluid. While an entity touches honey it gets Slowness III, and for a short while after.

Make a bucket of honey from three honey bottles and a bucket. The bottles are returned empty.

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [honey_bottle, honey_bottle, honey_bottle, bucket]
result: minekea:containers/honey_bucket
```

Honey buckets can fill a cauldron or a [glass jar](../02-containers/03-glass-jars.md).
