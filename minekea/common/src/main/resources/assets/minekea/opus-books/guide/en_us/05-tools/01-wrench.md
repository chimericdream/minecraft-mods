The wrench is the ultimate multi-tool.

```recipe
pattern: [" # ", " ##", "#  "]
key:
  "#": iron_ingot

```

## What it does

* Right-click a block to turn it. It cycles the facing or axis of blocks such as glazed terracotta, redstone
  components, stairs and logs.
* Right-click a slab to flip it between the top and the bottom of the block.
* Right-click a [beam](../01-building-blocks/01-beams.md) to switch the connection on that arm on or off.
* [Covers](../01-building-blocks/02-covers.md) can be rotated with it too.

Chests and beds are left alone.
