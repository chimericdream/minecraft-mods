What is fluffier than a block of wool? A pillow stuffed with four of them. Pillows come in all 16 colours, and you take no
fall damage when you land on one. Put a pillow in the crafting grid on its own to get four wool back.

```recipe
pattern: ["##", "##"]
key:
  "#": blue_wool
result: minekea:furniture/pillows/blue
```
