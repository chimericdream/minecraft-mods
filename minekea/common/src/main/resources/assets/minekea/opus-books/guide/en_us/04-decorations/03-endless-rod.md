Ever wish the end rod had no base? The End(less) Rod is an end rod without the base. Craft an end rod on its own to
get one, and an End(less) Rod on its own to turn it back into an end rod.

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [end_rod]
result: minekea:decorations/lighting/endless_rod
```
