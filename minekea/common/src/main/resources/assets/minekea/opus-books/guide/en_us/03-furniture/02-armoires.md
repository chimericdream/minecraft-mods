Armor stands are for peasants. An armoire stores up to four full sets of armour (16 slots) and is available in all wood
types.

```recipe
pattern: ["BSB", "BCB", "###"]
key:
  "#": oak_planks
  B: oak_slab
  C: chest
  S: armor_stand
result: minekea:furniture/armoires/oak
```
