These lanterns do not work any differently from vanilla ones, but they help when you need just the right ambiance.
Each uses iron nuggets, a torch, and one special ingredient in the middle.

## Ancient lantern

```recipe
pattern: ["###", "#P#", "#T#"]
key:
  "#": iron_nugget
  P: echo_shard
  T: torch
result: minekea:decorations/lighting/ancient_lantern
```
