summary: Barrels in every wood type.
---
Barrels are now available in all wood types. The recipe is material-specific, but is otherwise the same as the vanilla
barrel. They hold the same amount as a vanilla barrel.

> [!NOTE]
> The vanilla barrel texture is slightly modified so that it blends with the new variants.

```recipe
pattern: ["PSP", "P P", "PSP"]
key:
  P: spruce_planks
  S: spruce_slab
result: minekea:containers/barrels/spruce
```
