Covers look and behave almost like carpets, but in your choice of building material. They can be placed in different
directions, and a [wrench](../05-tools/01-wrench.md) rotates them. There are covers for well over a hundred
materials, and they all use the same recipe.

```recipe
pattern: ["# #", "   ", "# #"]
key:
  "#": cobblestone
result: minekea:building/covers/cobblestone
count: 16
```
