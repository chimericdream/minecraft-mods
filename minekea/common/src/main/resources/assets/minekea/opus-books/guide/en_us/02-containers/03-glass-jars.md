Jars can store a fluid, or a large amount of one kind of item. Most "dust" blocks, dyes, some foods and other items
can be stored in a jar. A jar keeps its contents when you break it, so it is a handy way to carry large quantities
around.

```recipe
pattern: [" L ", "G G", "GGG"]
key:
  L: [oak_planks, spruce_planks, birch_planks, jungle_planks, acacia_planks, dark_oak_planks]
  G: glass_pane
result: minekea:containers/glass_jar
count: 3
```

Any wooden planks work for the lid.

## Using a jar

* Right-click with a bucket of water, lava, milk or honey to pour it in. Right-click with an empty bucket to take it back.
* A jar holds up to 8 buckets of fluid. Water and honey bottles can be poured in and filled from the jar with glass
  bottles.
* Right-click with an item to put it in the jar. A jar holds eight stacks of one kind of item.
