Chairs and footstools come in all wood types, in plain and stripped styles. Right-click one to sit down.

Sneak and right-click an empty chair to turn it a quarter turn.

```recipe
pattern: ["P ", "PP", "LL"]
key:
  L: oak_log
  P: oak_planks
result: minekea:furniture/seating/chairs/oak
count: 2
```

```recipe
pattern: ["PP", "LL"]
key:
  L: oak_log
  P: oak_planks
result: minekea:furniture/seating/stools/oak
count: 2
```

For a stripped chair or stool, use the stripped log.
