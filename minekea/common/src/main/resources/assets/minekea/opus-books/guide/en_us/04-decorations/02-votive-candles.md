For something fancier than plain candles, stick them on a tiny glass pedestal. Votive candles work just like their vanilla
counterparts. Craft four candles of one colour (or plain candles) with a piece of glass to get four votive candles.

```recipe
type: crafting_shapeless
ingredients: [blue_candle, blue_candle, blue_candle, blue_candle, glass]
result: minekea:decorations/candles/blue_votive_candle
count: 4
```
