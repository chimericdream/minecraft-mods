Welcome to Minekea(tm)! In this catalogue you will find a wide range of building blocks, furniture, storage and
tools. (Some assembly required.)

Some families have a hundred or more blocks, so a recipe viewer such as Roughly Enough Items is a big help. The recipes
in this book show one example for each family; the others follow the same pattern with a different material.

## Where to start

* [Building blocks](01-building-blocks/index.md): beams, covers, vertical slabs and stairs, compressed blocks, dyed
  blocks, and more.
* [Containers and storage](02-containers/index.md): barrels, crates and glass jars.
* [Furniture](03-furniture/index.md): bookshelves, armoires, seats, tables, shelves, display cases, pillows and shutters.
* [Decorations](04-decorations/index.md): lanterns, votive candles, the End(less) Rod and fake cake.
* [Tools](05-tools/index.md): the wrench, hammers and block painter.
* [Crops and fluids](06-crops-and-fluids/index.md): warped wart, honey and milk.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your catalogue? Craft a new one from a book and an iron ingot.
