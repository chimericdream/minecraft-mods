---
title: Shutters
icon: item:minekea:furniture/shutters/oak
tags: [furniture]
summary: Window shutters that swing open a full 180 degrees.
---
Shutters work like trapdoors and come in all wood types, in plain and stripped styles. They open outward from the middle
of the block, a full 180 degrees, and can be waterlogged for interesting decorative effects.

```recipe
pattern: ["#X#", "#X#", "#X#"]
key:
  "#": oak_log
  X: oak_planks
result: minekea:furniture/shutters/oak
count: 6
```
