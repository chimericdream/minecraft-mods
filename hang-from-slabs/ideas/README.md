Feature ideas for this mod, from first thought to ready to build. The process is described in
[`docs/FEATURE-WORK.md`](../../docs/FEATURE-WORK.md).

## Identity

**Hang things from, and set things on, the surfaces vanilla refuses.** Lanterns, hanging signs, and their
vanilla kin attach to the flat faces of partial blocks, such as the underside of a top slab or the top of a
bottom slab. Vanilla only allows this on full-size blocks. It should feel like vanilla always worked this way,
with no new blocks, items, or recipes. It starts with vanilla blocks and leaves room for modded ones later.
