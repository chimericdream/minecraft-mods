---
title: Log All the Things!
---
Waterlogging lets water sit inside a slab or a fence. This mod teaches blocks a few more tricks: hold lava, hold a
window pane, hold a carpet, or hold a pile of snow. There is nothing new to craft. You just use items you already
have on blocks you already have.

## Where to start

* [Lava-logging](01-lava-logging.md): fill slabs, stairs, walls, fences, and more with lava, using a lava bucket.
* [Window-logging](02-window-logging.md): fit a glass pane or bars into a slab or stairs.
* [Carpet-logging](03-carpet-logging.md): lay a carpet inside a block instead of on top of it.
* [Snow-logging](04-snow-logging.md): build up layers of snow inside a block.
* [Tips and limits](05-tips.md): sneaking, what can be combined, and a quick reference.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a lava bucket (you keep the empty bucket).
