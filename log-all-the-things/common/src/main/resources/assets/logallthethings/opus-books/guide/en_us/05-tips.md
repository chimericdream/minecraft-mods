## Sneak to skip it

Every trick here steps aside when you sneak. Sneak while using a bucket, pane, carpet, or snow to place it the
normal way.

## Quick reference

| Item in hand | Click on | Result |
|:-------------|:---------|:-------|
| Lava bucket | Slab, stairs, wall, fence, bars, pane, trapdoor, ladder, chain (not flammable) | Fills with lava |
| Empty bucket | A lava-logged block | Takes the lava back |
| Glass pane or bars | Slab or straight stairs | Fits in as a window |
| Carpet | Slab, straight stairs, wall, fence, chain, bars, pane | Lays in as carpet |
| Snow | Same blocks as carpet | Adds a layer |

## Combining

* Lava and windows work together on a slab or stairs.
* Carpet and snow do not share a block.
* Windows and carpet do not share a block.

## Pick block

Aim at the pane, carpet, or snow and use pick block to get that item. Aim at the rest of the block to get the
slab, stairs, or other block underneath.
