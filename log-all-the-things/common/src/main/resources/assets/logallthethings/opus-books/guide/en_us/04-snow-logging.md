Right-click a block while holding snow to place it inside the block. Keep clicking to add more layers, the same
way you stack snow on the ground.

```item
id: minecraft:snow
```

## What works

Slabs, straight stairs, walls, fences, chains, bars, and glass panes (plain or stained), the same blocks
[carpet-logging](03-carpet-logging.md) works on.

## Layers

* Each click adds one layer.
* Most blocks hold up to 7 layers.
* Slabs and stairs hold up to 4, which is half a block.
* When a block is full, your next click places the snow the usual way.

## Taking it back

* Mine the snow to pop all of it back out at once. Aim at it to do this. Like normal snow, you need a shovel.
* Mine the solid part to break the whole thing.

## Limits

* Double slabs and corner stairs do not take snow.
* A block can hold snow or a carpet, not both.
* Sneak while using the snow to place it as a normal block instead.
