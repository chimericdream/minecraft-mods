Right-click a block while holding a carpet and the carpet is laid into it, instead of being placed on top. Slabs
and stairs get a neat carpeted surface, and fences and walls wear it too.

![White carpet](item:minecraft:white_carpet) ![Red carpet](item:minecraft:red_carpet) ![Moss carpet](item:minecraft:moss_carpet)

## What works

* Slabs and stairs
* Walls and fences
* Chains
* Bars and glass panes (plain or stained)
* Any carpet, including moss and pale moss carpet

## Taking it back

* Mine the carpet to pop just the carpet back out. Aim at it to do this.
* Mine the solid part to break the whole thing.

## Limits

* Double slabs do not take a carpet.
* Stairs must be straight, not a corner.
* A block can hold a carpet or snow, not both. See [snow-logging](04-snow-logging.md).
* Sneak while using the carpet to place it as a normal block instead.
