### Unreleased changes

#### New Features

* Added lava-logging: right-click a slab, stairs, wall, fence, iron bars/glass pane, trapdoor, ladder,
  or chain made of a non-flammable material with a lava bucket to fill it with lava, the same way
  water-logging already works. Empty the bucket on it again to pick the lava back up.
* Added window-logging: right-click a slab or plain stairs (not the corner-shaped ones) with a glass
  pane (any color) to fit the pane into the empty part of the block, turning it into a window. Aim at
  the glass while mining to pop just the pane back out; mining the solid part breaks the whole thing
  and drops both items.
