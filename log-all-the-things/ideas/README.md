## Identity

> Generalize vanilla's waterlogging mechanic to other fluids/materials.

### Boundary with Sponj

Sponj also deals with fluids, from a different direction.

- **This mod owns** fluids held inside blocks: the waterlogging mechanic extended to other fluids and
  materials.
- **Sponj owns** getting fluids out of the world and moving them around: absorbing, draining, drying, and
  reusing.
- **Not decided:** a sponge that absorbs a fluid from a logged block. Which mod owns that interaction, and
  whether one needs the other as a dependency?
