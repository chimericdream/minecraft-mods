# Window-logging

> Source: [`brainstorms/2026-08-30-legacy/`](../brainstorms/2026-08-30-legacy/potential-features.md) · Status: **Shipped** in 26.2-1.0.0

## Description

"Window-log" a slab or stairs block to make a window. Right-click with a pane to fit it in; aim at the glass
while mining to pop just the pane back out, or mine the solid part to break the whole thing. See
`CHANGELOG.md`.
