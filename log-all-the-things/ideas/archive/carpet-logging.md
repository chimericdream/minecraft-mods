> Source: [`brainstorms/2026-08-30-legacy/`](../brainstorms/2026-08-30-legacy/potential-features.md) · Status: **Shipped** in 26.2-1.0.0

## Description

"Carpet-log" a slab or plain stairs block. Right-click with a carpet to lay it in; aim at the carpet while
mining to pop just it back out, or mine the solid part to break the whole thing. See `CHANGELOG.md`.
