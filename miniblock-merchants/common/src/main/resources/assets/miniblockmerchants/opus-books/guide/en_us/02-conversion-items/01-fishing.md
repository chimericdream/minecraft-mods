Six conversion items can be fished up. They join the usual **treasure** catches, such as enchanted books and
saddles, so you need to land a treasure first (it helps to fish with Luck of the Sea).

| Item | Profession | Default chance |
|:-----|:-----------|---------------:|
| ![](item:miniblockmerchants:crystal_phial) Crystal Phial | [Alchemist](../03-professions/04-home-and-play.md#alchemist) | 1 in 24 |
| ![](item:miniblockmerchants:mixology_station) Mixology Station | [Bartender](../03-professions/01-food-and-drink.md#bartender) | 1 in 24 |
| ![](item:miniblockmerchants:drenched_score_sheet) Drenched Score Sheet | [Gamemaster](../03-professions/04-home-and-play.md#gamemaster) | 1 in 24 |
| ![](item:miniblockmerchants:soaked_villager_plushie) Soaked Villager Plushie | [Plushie Maniac](../03-professions/04-home-and-play.md#plushie-maniac) | 1 in 24 |
| ![](item:miniblockmerchants:rotting_recycling_bin) Rotting Recycling Bin | [Recycler](../03-professions/04-home-and-play.md#recycler) | 1 in 24 |
| ![](item:miniblockmerchants:forgotten_scrap_metal) Forgotten Scrap Metal | [Steampunker](../03-professions/03-stone-ore-and-metal.md#steampunker) | 1 in 24 |
