Welcome to the Miniblock Merchants guide. This mod adds 26 new villager professions that sell nearly 1,000
tiny decorative blocks, called miniblocks, for you to dress up your builds with.

```entity
minecraft:villager
```

There are no job blocks to place. Instead, you find a **conversion item** out in the world and use it on an
ordinary villager, and that villager becomes a miniblock merchant.

## Where to start

* [Getting started](01-getting-started/index.md): converting a villager, trading, and what you get.
* [Conversion items](02-conversion-items/index.md): where to find the item for each profession.
* [Professions](03-professions/index.md): what every merchant sells.
