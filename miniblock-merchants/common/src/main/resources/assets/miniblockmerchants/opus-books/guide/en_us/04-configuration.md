---
Every conversion item has its own drop chance, written as **1 in x**. A smaller number means the item drops
more often: **1** means every time, **2** means about half the time.

Open the settings from your mod list (on Fabric, through Mod Menu), under **Miniblock Merchants Config**. The
settings are also saved in `config/miniblockmerchants.json5`.

There is one option per item, for example "Crystal Phial Chance (1 in x)". The defaults are listed on the
[conversion items](02-conversion-items/index.md) page.

> [!TIP] Making it easier
> For survival, lowering the two 1 in 4096 items (Cultivated Sapling and Enchanted Red Delicious) is the
> biggest time saver.
