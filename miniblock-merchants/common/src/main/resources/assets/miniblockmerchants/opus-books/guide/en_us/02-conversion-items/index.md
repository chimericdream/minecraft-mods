Each profession has one conversion item. They are found by doing something ordinary in the world: fishing,
opening chests, breaking certain blocks, or defeating a creeper. The chance is usually small, so be patient.

Items are also in the **Functional Blocks** tab of the Creative inventory.

* [Fishing](01-fishing.md)
* [Chests](02-chests.md)
* [Blocks and creepers](03-blocks-and-mobs.md)
