There are 26 merchants and nearly 1,000 miniblocks in all. Every miniblock costs one emerald. The pages below
group the merchants by theme, with a few things each one sells.

* [Food and drink](01-food-and-drink.md): Baker, Bartender, Beekeeper, Chef, Olericulturist, Pomologist
* [Nature and outdoors](02-nature-and-outdoors.md): Arboriculturist, Astronomer, Eremologist, Horticulturist,
  Netherographer, Oceanographer
* [Stone, ore, and metal](03-stone-ore-and-metal.md): Blacksmith, Engineer, Mineralogist, Petrologist, Sculptor,
  Steampunker
* [Home and play](04-home-and-play.md): Alchemist, Furnisher, Gamemaster, Griefer, Plushie Maniac, Recycler,
  Ritualist, Tailor
