The rarest conversion items come from everyday actions. Odds are low, so this is a long game.

| Do this | Item | Profession | Default chance |
|:--------|:-----|:-----------|---------------:|
| Break oak leaves | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:enchanted_red_delicious) Enchanted Red Delicious | Pomologist | 1 in 4096 |
| Break spruce leaves | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:cultivated_sapling) Cultivated Sapling | Arboriculturist | 1 in 4096 |
| Break wheat | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:shimmering_wheat) Shimmering Wheat | Baker | 1 in 512 |
| Break carrots | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:overgrown_carrot) Overgrown Carrot | Olericulturist | 1 in 512 |
| Break redstone ore | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:radiating_redstone) Radiating Redstone | Engineer | 1 in 256 |
| Break clay | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:sculpting_clay) Sculpting Clay | Sculptor | 1 in 256 |
| Defeat a creeper | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:stabilized_explosion) Stabilized Explosion | Griefer | 1 in 160 |

> [!TIP]
> If waiting on a 1 in 4096 drop is not your idea of fun, lower the number in the
> [configuration](../04-configuration.md).
