Miniblock Merchants adds its own advancement tab.

| Advancement | How to earn it |
|:------------|:---------------|
| This looks interesting... | Get a conversion item. |
| Miniblock Connoisseur | Get every conversion item. |
| I know just where to put this! | Trade with a miniblock merchant. |
| A little bit of everything | Trade with every miniblock merchant. |
| Apprentice Decorator | Buy 100 items from miniblock merchants. |
| Experienced Decorator | Buy 250 items. |
| Remarkable Decorator | Buy 500 items. |
| Incredible Decorator | Buy 1,000 items. |
| Spectacular Decorator | Buy 5,000 items. |
| Legendary Decorator | Buy 10,000 items, then build more storage. |

Your running total is the **Traded with Miniblock Merchants** statistic in the Statistics screen.
