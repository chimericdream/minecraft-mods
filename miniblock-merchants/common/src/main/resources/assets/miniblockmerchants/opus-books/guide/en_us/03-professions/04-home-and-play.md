Miniblocks for living rooms, game nights, and the occasional explosion.

## Alchemist {#alchemist}

![](item:miniblockmerchants:crystal_phial) Conversion item: [Crystal Phial](../02-conversion-items/01-fishing.md)

Lab supplies: Cauldron, Gunpowder, Spider Eye, jars of many colors, Slime in a Bottle, Brain in a Bottle (16 trades).

## Furnisher {#furnisher}

![](item:miniblockmerchants:endless_bookshelf) Conversion item: [Endless Bookshelf](../02-conversion-items/02-chests.md)

Furniture and shelf dressing: chests in many materials, Barrel, Crate, stools, nightstand, stacks of books, lanterns, goblets (69 trades).

## Gamemaster {#gamemaster}

![](item:miniblockmerchants:drenched_score_sheet) Conversion item: [Drenched Score Sheet](../02-conversion-items/01-fishing.md)

Game pieces: colored Player Pieces, Creeper Pieces, coins, medals, dice, and chess pieces and boards in Diamond and Netherite (64 trades).

## Griefer {#griefer}

![](item:miniblockmerchants:stabilized_explosion) Conversion item: [Stabilized Explosion](../02-conversion-items/03-blocks-and-mobs.md)

Safe-to-hold explosives: Bomb, Grenade, Smoke Bomb, TNT, TNT Minecart, Fire Charge, Firework Rocket (12 trades).

## Plushie Maniac {#plushie-maniac}

![](item:miniblockmerchants:soaked_villager_plushie) Conversion item: [Soaked Villager Plushie](../02-conversion-items/01-fishing.md)

Plushies of Steve, Alex, Creeper, Enderman, Fox, Ghast, several villagers, and many more (66 trades).

## Recycler {#recycler}

![](item:miniblockmerchants:rotting_recycling_bin) Conversion item: [Rotting Recycling Bin](../02-conversion-items/01-fishing.md)

Sells a miniblock copy of every conversion item, from the Ancient Shell to the Wagyu Beef (26 trades). Handy for display shelves.

## Ritualist {#ritualist}

![](item:miniblockmerchants:book_of_rituals) Conversion item: [Book of Rituals](../02-conversion-items/02-chests.md)

Runes and orbs: Water, Earth, Fire, and Air Runes, Enchantment Rune, Lightning Rune, Rune Sack, and elemental Orbs (38 trades). Read the message this merchant sends when you convert them.

## Tailor {#tailor}

![](item:miniblockmerchants:fine_thread) Conversion item: [Fine Thread](../02-conversion-items/02-chests.md)

Clothes and cloth: Steve's and Alex's Clothes, piles of shirts and pants, scarves, backpacks, spools of thread in many colors, towels (35 trades).
