A miniblock is a player head with a custom texture, so it places as a tiny decorative model of a cake, a
cactus, a robot, or whatever the merchant sells.

* Place them like any head: on the ground, on a wall, or in an item frame.
* When you break a placed miniblock, it drops itself with its texture and name intact.
* Their names show in the tooltip, for example "Cauldron with Blood" or "Rainbow Cake".

## Merchant heads

When a **player** kills a miniblock merchant, you can get a head of that merchant:

| Mob | Drop |
|:----|:-----|
| Miniblock merchant | Always drops a head of that profession, for example "Alchemist". |
| Zombie version of a merchant | A head such as "Zombie Alchemist", 50% of the time (a little more with Looting). |

Placed on top of a note block, these heads play a villager sound (or a zombie villager sound for the zombie
heads).
