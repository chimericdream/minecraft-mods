These conversion items can appear in the chests of vanilla structures. The chance is per chest, and the
**Galilean Spyglass** and **Prismatic Honeycomb** are in every chest of their kind.

| Structure | Item | Profession | Default chance |
|:----------|:-----|:-----------|---------------:|
| Igloo | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:galilean_spyglass) Galilean Spyglass | Astronomer | Always |
| Jungle temple | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:prismatic_honeycomb) Prismatic Honeycomb | Beekeeper | Always |
| Village armorer | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:mastercrafted_iron) Mastercrafted Iron | Blacksmith | 1 in 2 |
| Village butcher | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:wagyu_beef) Wagyu Beef | Chef | 1 in 2 |
| Village shepherd | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:fine_thread) Fine Thread | Tailor | 1 in 2 |
| Desert pyramid | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:budding_cactus) Budding Cactus | Eremologist | 1 in 12 |
| Stronghold library | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:endless_bookshelf) Endless Bookshelf | Furnisher | 1 in 2 |
| Stronghold library | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:book_of_rituals) Book of Rituals | Ritualist | 1 in 2 |
| Pillager outpost | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:fragrant_flower) Fragrant Flower | Horticulturist | 1 in 2 |
| Monster room (dungeon) | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:pure_gold) 24-Karat Gold | Mineralogist | 1 in 4 |
| Ruined portal | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:sparkling_blaze_powder) Sparkling Blaze Powder | Netherographer | 1 in 4 |
| Big underwater ruin | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:ancient_shell) Ancient Shell | Oceanographer | 1 in 2 |
| Abandoned mineshaft | ![](item:miniblockmerchants:unusually_dense_rock) Unusually Dense Rock | Petrologist | 1 in 12 |

> [!NOTE]
> The stronghold library has two items to find: one for the Furnisher and one for the Ritualist.
