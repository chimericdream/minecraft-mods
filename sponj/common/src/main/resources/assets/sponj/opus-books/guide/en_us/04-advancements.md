Sponj has its own advancement tab with four goals:

| Advancement | How to earn it |
|:------------|:---------------|
| Big Gulp | Dry a region with 16 or more connected sponj |
| Spill Response Team | Absorb a total of 100,000 blocks of water |
| Dry Heat | Dry a wet sponj in the Nether |
| Space Heater | Dry a wet lava sponj in the End |

Sponj work on their own, so the advancement goes to everyone standing within 16 blocks when it happens.

Your running water total is also in the Statistics screen as **Water Blocks Absorbed**.
