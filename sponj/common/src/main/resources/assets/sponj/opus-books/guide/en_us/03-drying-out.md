Wet sponj do not soak up anything. Dry them to use them again.

## Wet Sponj

Two ways to dry a wet sponj:

* **Place it in the Nether.** It dries as soon as it is placed, with a hiss and a puff of steam.
* **Smelt it** in a furnace.

```recipe
type: smelting
ingredient: sponj:wet_sponj
result: sponj:sponj
cooking_time: 200
experience: 0.15
```

## Wet Lava Sponj

Place it in **the End**. It dries as soon as it is placed and turns back into a Lava Sponj. There is no smelting
recipe for it.

> [!TIP]
> Carry wet sponj in your inventory and place them in the right dimension to reset a whole stack quickly.
