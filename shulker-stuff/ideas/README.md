# Shulker Stuff — Ideas

Feature ideas for this mod, from first thought to ready to build. The process is described in
[`docs/FEATURE-WORK.md`](../../docs/FEATURE-WORK.md).

## Identity

**Shulker boxes, made more powerful and more pleasant to use.** Enchantments, upgrades, dyeing, and handling
tweaks, all recognizably shulker-flavored. Shulker boxes are the heart of it, but anything shulker-related is
fair game, including the mob and its drops. It is vanilla-friendly and additive: existing boxes keep working as
they are, and every change is an opt-in extra.
