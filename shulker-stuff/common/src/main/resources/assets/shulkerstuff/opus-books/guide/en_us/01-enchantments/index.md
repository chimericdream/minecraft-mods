Shulker Stuff adds three enchantments that only work on shulker boxes:

* [Refill](01-refill.md): swaps in a fresh stack when the one in your hand runs out.
* [Vacuum](02-vacuum.md): sends picked-up items straight into the box.
* [Void](03-void.md): destroys picked-up items you already have in the box.

You can find them in the enchanting table, in villager trades, and as treasure. Hover over an enchanted book to
read what it does.

The box has to be **in your inventory** for any of them to work. Placing it on the ground turns them off.

> [!NOTE]
> A *Deep Storage* enchantment is planned but does nothing yet, so it can't be obtained.
