**Void** (level I) destroys items you pick up that match something already in the box.

Use it to stop junk filling your inventory. Put one cobblestone or one piece of rotten flesh in the box, and from
then on every one you pick up disappears.

## Good to know

* The box itself is the filter, so what you put inside decides what is destroyed.
* The destroyed items are gone for good. Think before you put anything valuable in.
* If a box has both [Vacuum](02-vacuum.md) and Void, Vacuum wins and Void does nothing.
* Items that can't be stored in a shulker box are never destroyed.
