**Refill** (level I) refills your hand from the box when the stack you are holding runs out.

## How it works

Use up the last block, torch, or arrow stack in your main hand or off hand, and a matching stack is taken from the
shulker box and put in that slot. The items must match exactly, so a plain stone block will not replace a renamed
one.

Refill takes the first matching stack it finds in the box, and checks every Refill box in your inventory.

## When it does not work

* In creative mode.
* While a menu is open, such as your inventory or a chest.
* Right after you change hotbar slots or swap hands.

> [!TIP]
> Pack a box with building blocks and carry it for long building sessions, so you never have to stop and restock.
