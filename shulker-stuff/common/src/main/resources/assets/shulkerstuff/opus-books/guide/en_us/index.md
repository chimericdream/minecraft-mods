title: Shulker Stuff
---
Welcome to the Shulker Stuff guide. This mod makes shulker boxes more useful and nicer to look at: enchant them to
refill your hand or soak up loot, dye the lid and base separately, and plate them in netherite.

## Where to start

* [Enchantments](01-enchantments/index.md): Refill, Vacuum, and Void.
* [Shulker Dyeing Station](02-dye-station.md): recolor the lid and base of a shulker box.
* [Netherite Plating](03-netherite-plating.md): make a shulker box safe from explosions and fire.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and a shulker shell.
