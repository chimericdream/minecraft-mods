**Vacuum** (levels I and II) sends items you pick up into the box instead of your inventory.

## Levels

| Level | What it collects |
|:------|:-----------------|
| I | Items that match something already in the box, topping up its existing stacks. |
| II | Any item that fits, using empty slots too. |

Anything that is left over, because the box was full, goes into your inventory as normal.

## Good to know

* Items that can't be stored in a shulker box, such as other shulker boxes, are ignored.
* If a box has both Vacuum and [Void](03-void.md), Vacuum wins.
* Vacuum does not work on a stack of shulker boxes. If you are using a mod that lets them stack, you will see a
  notice above the hotbar and the items go into your inventory instead.

> [!TIP]
> Put one of each ore or block you mine into the box and use Vacuum I to collect just those.
