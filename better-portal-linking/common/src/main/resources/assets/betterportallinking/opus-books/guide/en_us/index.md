---
Welcome to the Better Portal Linking guide. In vanilla Minecraft a nether portal always leads to whichever portal is
nearest. With this mod you decide: put matching blocks on the corners of two portal frames and those portals link to
each other.

Portals without corner blocks work exactly like vanilla, so nothing changes until you decorate one.

## Where to start

* [Portal addresses](01-portal-addresses.md): what an address is and how to build one.
* [How links are chosen](02-how-links-are-chosen.md): matching, ties, and what happens when nothing matches.
* [Settings and tips](03-settings-and-tips.md): the config screen, changing which blocks count, and building advice.
