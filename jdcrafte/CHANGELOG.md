### Unreleased changes

#### New Features

* Added the feeding trough, a block that fills up as feed (wheat, beetroot, carrots, potatoes, or
  wheat seeds) is added. Right-click with feed in hand to fill it, or shift-right-click with an
  empty hand to take feed back out.
* Added the weathervane, a decorative rooftop weather vane. Right-click to spin it.
* Added the trellis, a climbable decorative wooden lattice. Comes in every wood type:
  * Acacia Trellis
  * Bamboo Trellis
  * Birch Trellis
  * Cherry Trellis
  * Crimson Trellis
  * Dark Oak Trellis
  * Jungle Trellis
  * Mangrove Trellis
  * Oak Trellis
  * Pale Oak Trellis
  * Spruce Trellis
  * Warped Trellis
  * Stripped Acacia Trellis
  * Stripped Bamboo Trellis
  * Stripped Birch Trellis
  * Stripped Cherry Trellis
  * Stripped Crimson Trellis
  * Stripped Dark Oak Trellis
  * Stripped Jungle Trellis
  * Stripped Mangrove Trellis
  * Stripped Oak Trellis
  * Stripped Pale Oak Trellis
  * Stripped Spruce Trellis
  * Stripped Warped Trellis
* Added the trellis arch, a climbable, 3-block-wide decorative archway crafted from 3 trellises.
  Comes in the same 24 wood types as the trellis - crafting a trellis arch takes 3 trellises of
  that same type.
