### Unreleased changes

#### New Features

* Added the feeding trough, a block that fills up as feed (wheat, beetroot, carrots, potatoes, or
  wheat seeds) is added. Right-click with feed in hand to fill it, or shift-right-click with an
  empty hand to take feed back out.
* Added the weathervane, a decorative rooftop weather vane. Right-click to spin it.
* Added the trellis, a climbable decorative wooden lattice. Comes in every wood type:
  * Acacia Trellis
  * Bamboo Trellis
  * Birch Trellis
  * Cherry Trellis
  * Crimson Trellis
  * Dark Oak Trellis
  * Jungle Trellis

  * Spruce Trellis
