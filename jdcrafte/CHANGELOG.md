### Unreleased changes

#### New Features

* The feeding trough now helps your animals breed. Every 10 seconds, if it holds food that cows, sheep, pigs, chickens, rabbits, or horses like and two of the same kind are nearby, it feeds them a pair (one food item each) so they pair up on their own. It does nothing in a crowded pen (24 or more animals). What you stock decides who breeds: wheat for cows and sheep, carrots for pigs and rabbits, wheat seeds for chickens, golden carrots for horses and rabbits, golden apples for horses.
* Troughs now also accept golden carrots and golden apples.
* Added a JD Crafte config (JD Crafte settings in Mod Menu) to turn trough feeding off or change its range, speed, and crowding limit.
* Added the JD Crafte Guide, an in-game book covering the feeding trough, weathervane, trellises, and Home Sweet Hrmmm plaque. Craft one from a book and wheat, or use `/opus give jdcrafte:guide`. Needs the optional Opus mod (1.2.0 or newer).
* Added the feeding trough, a block that fills up as feed (wheat, beetroot, carrots, potatoes, or
  wheat seeds) is added. Right-click with feed in hand to fill it, or shift-right-click with an
  empty hand to take feed back out.
* Added the weathervane, a decorative rooftop weather vane. Right-click to spin it.
* Added the Home Sweet Hrmmm plaque, a two-block-wide decorative wall sign. Comes in every wood type:
  * Acacia
  * Bamboo
  * Birch
  * Cherry
  * Crimson
  * Dark Oak
  * Jungle
  * Mangrove
  * Oak
  * Pale Oak
  * Spruce
  * Warped
* Added the trellis, a climbable decorative wooden lattice, as well as trellis arch. Comes in every wood type:
  * Acacia
  * Bamboo
  * Birch
  * Cherry
  * Crimson
  * Dark Oak
  * Jungle
  * Mangrove
  * Oak
  * Pale Oak
  * Spruce
  * Warped
  * All stripped variants
