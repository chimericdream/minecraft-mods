### Unreleased changes

#### New Features

* Added the JD Crafte Guide, an in-game book covering the feeding trough, weathervane, trellises, and Home Sweet Hrmmm plaque. Craft one from a book and wheat, or use `/opus give jdcrafte:guide`.
* Opus 1.2.0 or newer is now required (it provides the guide book).
* Added the feeding trough, a block that fills up as feed (wheat, beetroot, carrots, potatoes, or
  wheat seeds) is added. Right-click with feed in hand to fill it, or shift-right-click with an
  empty hand to take feed back out.
* Added the weathervane, a decorative rooftop weather vane. Right-click to spin it.
* Added the Home Sweet Hrmmm plaque, a two-block-wide decorative wall sign. Comes in every wood type:
  * Acacia
  * Bamboo
  * Birch
  * Cherry
  * Crimson
  * Dark Oak
  * Jungle
  * Mangrove
  * Oak
  * Pale Oak
  * Spruce
  * Warped
* Added the trellis, a climbable decorative wooden lattice, as well as trellis arch. Comes in every wood type:
  * Acacia
  * Bamboo
  * Birch
  * Cherry
  * Crimson
  * Dark Oak
  * Jungle
  * Mangrove
  * Oak
  * Pale Oak
  * Spruce
  * Warped
  * All stripped variants
