_One-stop shop for all your farming and agricultural needs!_

## Introduction

JD Crafte is about the quieter side of a Minecraft farm — the troughs, fences, and weathervanes
that make a homestead feel lived-in rather than just functional. Nothing here is essential to
surviving the game; it's all the rustic dressing that makes a farm worth showing off.

### Minecraft Versions

* 26.2: Supported

### Current Features

* **Feeding trough.** A block that holds feed (wheat, beetroot, carrots, potatoes, or wheat seeds)
  for your animals. Right-click it with feed in hand to fill it up, and it'll visibly fill as more
  is added; shift-right-click with an empty hand to take feed back out.
* **Weathervane.** A decorative rooftop weather vane. Right-click it to spin it in place.
