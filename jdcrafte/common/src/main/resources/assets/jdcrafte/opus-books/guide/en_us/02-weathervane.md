A decorative weather vane for the top of your barn or roof.

```recipe
pattern: ["F E", "III"]
key:
  F: flint
  E: egg
  I: iron_bars
result: jdcrafte:weathervane
```

The recipe takes any kind of egg.

## Spinning it

Right-click the weathervane to turn it one step (45 degrees). Shift-right-click to turn it the other way. It makes the same sound as turning an item in an item frame.
