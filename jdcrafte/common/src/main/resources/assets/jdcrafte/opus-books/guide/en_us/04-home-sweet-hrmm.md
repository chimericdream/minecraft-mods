The Home Sweet Hrmmm plaque is a wide wall sign for your front door. It comes in every wood type: Acacia, Bamboo, Birch, Cherry, Crimson, Dark Oak, Jungle, Mangrove, Oak, Pale Oak, Spruce, and Warped.

Craft an emerald over a sign of the matching wood.

```recipe
pattern: ["E", "S"]
key:
  E: emerald
  S: oak_sign
result: jdcrafte:oak_home_sweet_hrmm
```

## Placing it

The plaque is two blocks wide and sits flat against a wall. Click where the left side should go and the right side is placed beside it, to your right as you look at the wall. There has to be room for both halves.

Breaking either half removes the whole plaque, and it gives back one item.
