Welcome to the JD Crafte guide. This mod adds blocks that a working farm needs, and that make it feel like home.

## Where to start

* [Feeding trough](01-feeding-trough.md): a trough that holds feed and fills up as you add more.
* [Weathervane](02-weathervane.md): a rooftop weather vane you can spin.
* [Trellises](03-trellises.md): climbable wooden lattices and the arches that join them.
* [Home Sweet Hrmmm](04-home-sweet-hrmm.md): a two-block-wide wall plaque.

Everything is in the Functional Blocks creative tab.

> [!TIP]
> Lost your guide? Craft a new one from a book and some wheat.
